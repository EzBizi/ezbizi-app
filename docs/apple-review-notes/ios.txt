FUNCTION AND AUDIENCE
EzBizi is field-service business software for multi-location trades such as chimney sweeps and garage-door installers. Customers, jobs, inspections, inventory, vehicles and staff sit on the same records, scoped so each location sees its own work while the group above sees all of it. Audience: owners, office staff and field technicians at multi-location service businesses. Sold to the business, not to consumers.

ACCESS
One account type. Sign in with the credentials in the Sign-In Information fields. That account administers a demonstration organization holding invented data only - no real customer or business information. No setup, no sample files. The sidebar lists every area; account deletion is in the account screen, reached from the avatar.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
Registration: "Create an account" on the sign-in screen sets up a new business with its owner as administrator. Staff join by invitation from their organization. No payment is asked for or taken in the app.
No purchases, subscriptions or paid content. Nothing is sold in the app.
No public user content, feed or profiles. Everything is a private business record of that organization, and people can reach only colleagues in it (for example a coworker's phone extension).

PERMISSIONS
The app asks for three permissions, each only at the moment it is needed:
- Microphone: the first time someone makes or takes a phone call in the app, or starts a safety recording. A safety recording is started by the technician with a tap, only for staff whose employer allows it; it records the visit for the technician's safety and training, continues while the phone is locked (the audio background mode, with iOS's microphone indicator showing), and stops when they tap Stop, leave the job, or after four hours.
- Notifications: so a job assigned to someone reaches their phone.
- Location (When In Use only, never Always): the Field feature is off unless an employer turns it on, and applies only to employees whose role the employer marks as tracked. The employer chooses when: while clocked in, during scheduled hours, or whenever the app runs. It is used for staff safety, SOS, recording arrival at a job, and routing between jobs. Sharing starts only while the app is open; iOS then shows its blue location indicator whenever location is used in the background.
It does not use contacts, camera, photos or App Tracking Transparency.

ACCOUNT DELETION
A person deletes their own account from the account screen. It requires their password and typing DELETE, then erases their personal details and signs them out on every device. The sole administrator is refused until someone else is made administrator.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (hosting, database), Amazon SES (transactional email), WhatsApp Business by Meta (messaging).
Optional integrations, none in the demo, each connected by the customer with their own account and removable any time: Twilio (phone, SMS); Google and Microsoft 365 (company mailbox); Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, X, YouTube (publishing the customer's own posts to their own accounts); Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Thumbtack (lead sources).
No payment processor and no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK.
AI: call summaries, lead notes, review-reply drafts and the staff Assistant send the organization's own business records to xAI (Grok) to write that text. Results are shown only inside that organization; xAI is not used for advertising or tracking.
