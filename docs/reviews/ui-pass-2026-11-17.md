# UI pass — 2026-11-17 (overnight, desktop + phone)

**How this was done.** I used Jose's signed-in Chrome on production
(https://app.ezbizi.com, **v0.0.0577 · 49ec335c1**). Each screen was drawn in a
1440x900 frame and then in a 390x844 frame. Dark theme only: the theme is an
account preference, so I did not flip it. The pass was read-only. I opened tabs
and records only. The one click with an effect was "Not now" on the shift
reminder, which closes the reminder and writes nothing.

**Coverage.**
- **Desktop 1440.** Every module, opened from its URL, and its tab row. Opened
  in detail: Scheduling (all 7 tabs and 5 Settings pills), Jobs (both tabs and a
  Job record), Inspection (4), Field (5), Project Management (list and
  Settings), Customers (both tabs and the 4 Membership pills), Leads, Customer
  Experience (7), Financials (Home, Bills, Invoices, Settings), Communication
  (Phone, Users, Records, Reports, Alerts, and Settings › Connection, Numbers,
  Ring groups and Trust), Email, Marketing (Social Dashboard, Compose and
  Calendar; Newsletter; Web Management; Ads), Services, Fleet (2), Company,
  Inventory, Task Board (Table), HR (Dashboard), Work Hours (General), Training,
  Guidelines, AI Assistance, Reports (list, Scoring, Profit & Loss), Monitoring,
  Automations, Settings (Preferences) and Suggestions.
- **Phone 390.** Dashboard, Scheduling (Availability, Schedule, Re-Scheduling),
  Jobs (both tabs) and Reports (list and Scoring).
- **Stopped at 5:00 PM.** Jose's shift ended. Production then put the "Clock In
  / Continue without clocking in" lock screen over the whole app. Getting past it
  needs a POST (`/time-clock/skip-lock`), which this read-only pass may not send.
  The phone pass, the 1920 frame and the remaining sub-tabs have to wait for a
  session while Jose is clocked in.
- **Android emulator.** `UIUX_Phone` exists but was not used. Against production
  it would show the same lock screen.

Severity: **broken** = unusable, clipped or wrong · **ugly** = works but looks
wrong · **polish** = small. **FIXED** = fixed on `session/uiux-pass3` in
`87d36265c`. **CHOICE** = drawn in
`docs/previews/ui-pass-2026-11-17.html`.
