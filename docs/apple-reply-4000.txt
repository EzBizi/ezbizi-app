3. FUNCTION AND AUDIENCE
EzBizi is field-service business software for companies running work across more than one location - chimney sweeps, garage-door installers and similar trades with several branches or franchises.

The problem: the office and the field work from different systems. The customer record is in one place, the schedule in another, inspection notes on paper, the van's service history in a folder. Nobody answers a customer's question without asking three people.

EzBizi puts customers, jobs, inspections, inventory, vehicles and staff on the same records, scoped so each location sees its own work while the group above sees all of it. A customer served by two locations is still one customer.

Audience: owners, office staff and field technicians at multi-location service businesses. Sold to the business, not to consumers.

4. SETUP AND ACCESS

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (hosting, database), Amazon SES (transactional email).

Optional per-tenant integrations, none required and none configured in the demo organization. Each is connected by the customer with their own account and can be disconnected at any time: Twilio (phone, SMS); Google and Microsoft 365 (company mailbox); Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, X, YouTube (publishing the customer's own posts to their own accounts); Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Thumbtack (lead sources).

Stated because you ask: no payment processor, no AI service, and no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK.
