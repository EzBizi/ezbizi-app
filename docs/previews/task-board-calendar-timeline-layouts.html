Task Board: Calendar, Timeline, Number and Formula

From the Monday survey (A-1 off Monday by Nov 10). Ohio PM plans by date (Calendar, Gantt) and totals costs (Job Cost, Total Cost). Today the board has Table, Kanban and Cards only. Every name is invented.

1 Two new views in the existing switcher, columns in their settings panel RECOMMENDED

  • Calendar: tasks on their due date, coloured by status, Month | Week. Drag a task to change its date; tap opens it in the SidePanel. Tasks with no date sit in a "No date (N)" tray.
  • Timeline: a new Timeline column (from–to, one range picker) and a Gantt view. Rows follow the board's groups; bars drag and resize; today line; Week | Month | Quarter.
  • Number and Formula: Number gets a format (Plain, Currency, %). A Formula column is built in the column settings SidePanel. Group footers sum.
