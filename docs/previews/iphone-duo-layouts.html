206 · iPhone Duo: the open inner screen gets a tablet layout

Jose, 11-23, via the desk: "we need Iphone Duo support… it's not optimized for it." The Duo has two screens. Folded, the outer screen is about 466 pt wide, an ordinary phone, which we already handle. Open, the inner screen is about 669×951 pt upright and 951×669 sideways, close to an iPad mini. Today the open screen falls between our two layouts: no bottom bar, a hamburger menu, and phone screens stretched across it. Each option below shows the inner screen upright, then sideways ("book"). The grey line marks the fold. Flutter can't report the fold on iOS yet, so no control should sit on it. Leads is the example screen; HR Records, Customers and Email follow the same rule. Folding or unfolding keeps the open record.

1 · Keep the bottom bar, split list and record side by side (recommended)

Unfolding keeps what the thumb already knows: the same four modules and More at the bottom. The extra width goes to content, with the list on the left half and the open record on the right half, so the fold falls on the line between them. Side panels open at 420 pt from the right, leaving the list visible. Sideways (951 wide) it becomes today's desktop shell with the slim sidebar.
