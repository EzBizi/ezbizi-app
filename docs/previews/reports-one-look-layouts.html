Reports: one look for every tab

Jose asked to look at every tab in Reports, the new ones and the old, and make them better visually. Every tab and sub-tab was drawn with invented data on a computer and a phone (110 pictures). The tabs work, but each one is laid out its own way: there are three date controls, five table styles, four kinds of empty page, money written three ways, and side padding that changes from tab to tab. These options put every tab in one frame. Every name and number here is invented.

The frame, in every option: the tab strip, then one toolbar (the same date control on every tab, the tab's filters as small pickers, its actions on the right), then figures (equal tiles: money as $32,520.77, the change against the dates before), then one table style: plain-English headings, numbers right-aligned in even columns, the naming columns pinned while you scroll sideways, a Total row, losses in red, a dash for "none". A row opens in a panel beside the table. Empty pages are one kind: what is missing, and the one place to fix it.

Fixed whichever option is picked (they are bugs, not looks):
  • On a phone, Marketing, Meta and Thumbtack went blank when the dates were opened. Already fixed (in Scoring's build).
  • A saved report's run showed money as raw cents ("29320"); the Builder's preview of the same column says $1,708.20.
  • Performance wrote call times as 13:33 with the 12-hour setting on.
  • Google Ads wrote money as "32520.77 USD", with no thousands separator.

Marketing, as every tab would read. The date control is Performance's, now used everywhere, Google Ads and Profit & Loss included. Branch and Source stay put while the other twelve columns scroll. Total sits at the bottom, net profit is red when it is a loss, and the grey "Scroll for 6 more columns ›" says the table goes on.

4–6 Phone

Today on a phone, "New report" takes the strip's width, so only "Reports" and a clipped "Repor…" show. The other tabs hide behind a sideways scroll with no hint, and tables are wider than the screen. 4 (recommended): one Reports ▾ picker naming the tab you are on and listing all nine; the date as a full-width button; two figures a row; each table row as a card with its three main numbers, a tap opening the rest. 5: the strip kept, with a fade where it continues, and the table kept, its naming column pinned. 6: figures and the top five rows only, then "See all 14 rows".
