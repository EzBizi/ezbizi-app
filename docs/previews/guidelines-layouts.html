Guidelines, a new module

Jose, today: a module of guidelines, one card per situation. Each card has a colour and, optionally, a picture, and tapping one opens step-by-step instructions. A Settings tab creates, edits and deletes them. Every situation, name and step here is invented.

The Guidelines session builds the server: cards scoped to a place like the AI Library, with guidelines.view and guidelines.manage. A card has a title, a short summary, a colour from the app's own set, an optional picture, its steps in order, and optional notes. Search covers the title, summary and steps. A card's colour is a band and an icon tint, never a full fill, so the words read the same in dark mode.

1 A grid of cards; a card opens its steps in a panel RECOMMENDED

Search at the top, then the cards, all the same size: the colour band, an icon or picture, the title, one line of summary and how many steps. A click opens the steps in a side panel over the right of the grid, numbered in the card's colour, with the notes under them. The grid stays in view, so the next card is one click away.
