Jose, 2026-10-01: “we need also a layout redesign for the AI Assistance tab”.

Answer with one desktop number and one phone number, for example “1, 4”. Desktop is 1–3, phone is 4–6.

What is wrong today: the assistant’s name, its welcome, an Ask field and four folded rows (NFPA 211, CSIA, Finance options and an About section), each as wide as the screen with one word in it. The rest of a 2000px screen is empty. Nothing shows what a section holds until it is opened, and an answer is two clicks deep (NFPA 211, then Flue sizing), unfolding inside the list and pushing everything under it down. What the Ask field finds lands in the same column: answers as more folding rows, branches as plain lines of text. The map link the server sends with a branch is dropped, and there is no Call button. Bold headings in an answer are stripped instead of shown bold. Nothing marks the answers that are for staff only, and a question asked a minute ago is gone.

Every option keeps what works today: one Ask field running the WhatsApp line’s own search (Library answers first, then branches whose city, state, branch code, address or service area match), the Library’s sections and answers, and the name and welcome from Settings. Answers stay the Library’s own words. No AI writes them, as decided on 2026-09-25 (“build free for now”). Every option adds, client only: bold shown as bold. Each answer says where it comes from (NFPA 211 › Flue sizing) and keeps the standard’s footnote. Each branch is a card with Call, Map (the link already sent) and Copy. A section with one answer opens it straight away, as on WhatsApp. Today’s questions stay until the app closes. Keeping them between sign-ins would need a list preference on the server. The topic lists below are the Library’s answers, read-only. The Library tab still writes them in its side panel, and Edit in Library, for people who may edit, opens the answer there.

Shared and optional, on the server: the AI tab’s two calls (/ai-assistance/menu and /ask) also send each answer’s audience, which the Library tab already receives. That lets Staff only show where the answer is read. Without it, the pill is left out.

Flue sizing for a fireplace
Against the fireplace opening

NFPA 211 sizes the flue against the area of the fireplace opening:

  • Round flue: 1/12 of the opening area
  • Square or standard rectangular flue: 1/10
  • Rectangular, 2:1 or longer: 1/8

Example: a 36 × 30 in opening is 1,080 sq in, so a round flue needs at least 90 sq in.
