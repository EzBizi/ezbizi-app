Phone › IVR menus — layouts

Read from flows_pane.dart “What a caller hears before anybody picks up. A line with no menu rings its group.” A menu has a name, a spoken prompt — “Write what the caller hears” — and keys, each doing one of: Take a message, Forward the call, Say something and hang up, or ring a group. Its validations already exist and are good: “Two keys cannot do different things.” “Say which group each ringing key reaches.” “Say where each forwarding key sends the call.” Empty state kept: “No menus yet. Every line rings its group as soon as it is answered.”
The finding: a menu can be fully configured and never play, and this pane cannot say so General's chain skips the menu step when the menu has no usable step — that is TwimlRenderer::describe(), and Phone is adding a distinct skipped bool for it. **A key pointing at a deleted ring group, or a forwarding key with no destination, makes the whole menu unusable — and the caller simply never hears it.** Today that fact lives in General and in Call flow, and not here. You can open this pane, look at a menu with a name, a prompt and five configured keys, and have no way to learn that it never plays. The validations catch it while you are editing and then the knowledge evaporates: reopen the pane later and the menu looks finished. So every layout draws menu-level usability in the list, and marks the individual key that broke it. The same rule Ring groups just settled: a per-row incapacity belongs in the row, not in a banner — and here it belongs in two rows, the menu and the key.
One choice that is really about who uses this A menu is authored once by an owner and then read occasionally by whoever is debugging a complaint. Layout 1 optimises for authoring (a row per key, everything editable in place); layout 2 optimises for reading (a keypad you recognise at a glance, click a key to edit). If menus are set up once and rarely touched, 2 is the better screen and I would switch. That is a fact about your customers rather than about the design.

Pick a number. 1 is my recommendation. 4 is it at 375px.
