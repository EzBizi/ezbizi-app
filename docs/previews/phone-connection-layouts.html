Read from settings_tab.dart — Connection is inline, not its own file A connection is a Twilio account, held at the organization or at a company. It shows connected or “Not working”, whether it is “Your Twilio” or an “EzBizi phone account”, and the SID tail — “Account SID ends …a91. The key is never shown back.” When there is none, the action is “Activate phone service”. Read-only except for connecting and changing, so under the approved pane pattern the footer is null. Retry belongs in the head here — this pane's error is a failed load, not a refused save.
The finding: one inheritance rule, explained by six different sentences Company numbers fall back to the organization's connection unless that company has its own. That single rule is currently delivered as six prose variants, chosen by which state you happen to be in:
  • “Organization line. Switch to the group to connect or change it. Company numbers use this unless this company has its own.”
  • “Organization line. Switch to the group to change it. Company numbers use this unless this company has its own.”
  • “No connection. Company numbers use this unless that company has its own.”
  • “Numbers without their own company connection use this.”
  • “No connection. Numbers here use the organization until this exists.”
  • “This company has its own Twilio.”
Each is accurate. Together they are six things to keep in step, and none of them shows the shape. Somebody reading any one of them still has to build the two-level picture in their head — and the sentence they get depends on where they are standing, so the rule looks different from the organization than from a company. A structure says it once.
One thing worth your ruling The prose is good and I am not proposing to delete it — I am proposing to demote it. Layouts 1 and 3 draw the inheritance and keep one short sentence underneath; layout 2 keeps the sentences as the primary explanation and only tidies them. If you would rather this pane stay text-led because connections are rare and read by owners rather than staff, layout 2 is the honest version of that and I have not strawmanned it.
