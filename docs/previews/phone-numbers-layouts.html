Read from numbers_pane.dart — 1092 lines, the largest pane by a distance “The lines this business answers on, and what each one does.” The pane is doing five separate jobs: the line list, the import flow for numbers already on the Twilio account, a move panel that shifts a line to another company, the buy a number panel (its own file), and a per-line form that is inline here and is most of the bulk. Opening a line does not scroll — :273 returns _LineForm outright, so the form replaces the whole pane and the list disappears.
The finding: the same line settings are editable in two places, and five fields are identical General has a line dropdown (_lineId) — it edits one line at a time. Numbers' _LineForm edits a line too. Compared field by field:

Five fields are literally the same control in two panes, and the rest overlap in substance. That is not a layout problem and no arrangement fixes it — it is one fact with two editors, which is the condition where the two quietly disagree and nobody can say which is right. It also explains the 1092 lines.
So the real choice here is not how to arrange Numbers — it is whether Numbers edits lines at all 1 · Layout 1 says no. Numbers becomes the inventory: what lines exist, what each one does, import, move, buy. Editing a line opens General with that line selected — one editor, which is the screen you already approved as the chain. Deletes roughly 270 lines of duplicated form. 2 · Layouts 2 and 3 say yes, and keep both editors. They fix the replace-the-pane problem and leave the duplication in place. I have drawn them honestly rather than strawmanned them, because “two editors is fine if they are both good” is a real position — but somebody has to hold it deliberately rather than by accident. 3 · Whichever you pick, the silent-lines alarm moves to the top and names the line. The existing copy is already good — “Nobody answers this line. Every call goes straight to voicemail.” and “No ring groups exist yet, so there is nobody for a line to ring.” — but it currently sits below the list.
