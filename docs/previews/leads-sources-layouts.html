Leads › four sources — tabs, or one list?

Six numbered layouts: 1–3 desktop, 4–6 mobile. This one carries a recommendation that differs from how Jose worded it, which is stated plainly below rather than buried.

Jose said “a new sub tab in Leads” for each. I am recommending against it, and here is why that is not me ignoring him He said it while looking at a screen that has tabs, and describing an outcome: chat and forms should be visible in Leads. His own reference — a1tools, which he has had open beside this all week — puts every source in one list with the source as a chip across the top. So the one-list shape is arguably closer to what he has been pointing at than to what he typed. He also said, tonight: “if u have a question do as you believe or recommend, dont wait for me.” So layout 2 is his wording, drawn properly and buildable as-is. If he says “tabs” tomorrow it is one word and Leads builds 2 instead — the row, the badges and the sources band are identical in both, so only the strip changes.
Measured: today's two tabs are NOT a source split, which weakens the precedent The Thumbtack tab renders no leads at all — it shows a connect flow and the sentence “Connected — enquiries not fetched yet”. So the current strip is not “Google's leads / Thumbtack's leads”. It is the leads, and a provider that has none. Adding two more tabs would be the first time the strip actually sliced one list four ways. And that slicing breaks the question the whole triage design answers. The unread weight, the unreplied dot and the star were approved to answer “who is waiting on me”. Split across four tabs they answer it per source — and nobody has ever wanted to know who is waiting among Google leads specifically. Four tabs means four places to check before you know you are done. Source is also the FOURTH filter, and three already live together. Unread, Starred and Unreplied are one chip row that narrows together, approved and built. Put source in a tab strip and one idea is split across two controls, where "Website chat + Unread" is a tab plus a chip instead of two chips.
What the sources band becomes, because two providers is the real objection Google's accounts band works because there is one provider. With Thumbtack mounting the same list, two bands stacked above one list is worse than one band per tab — that is the honest cost of collapsing the strip, and it has an answer that already exists: one “Connected sources” row opening a panel, which is exactly the band→panel shape shipped for Google. One row whatever the number of providers, the same as it costs one row whatever the number of accounts. One small ask of Leads either way. index() has no source filter today — I read the query. Layouts 1 and 3 need source= on the list; layout 2 needs it too, since a tab is a filter with a different hat on. And the schema holds, checked while I was in there. The list already scopes by reachableFranchises and reachableCompanies, so nothing here changes who sees what.
