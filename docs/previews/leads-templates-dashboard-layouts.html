Leads · Templates and a Dashboard

Jose, 2026-09-30: “see if you can improve the leads template screen, it looks terrible”, then “also make a new tab called Dashboard which gathers info, reply rates from each user, response time, amounts of leads, graphs, etc” — “sub tab for leads i mean, like with the connections and templates sub tabs”.

Two questions on one page. Templates is 1–6 and Dashboard is 7–12. Answer with two desktop numbers and two phone numbers, for example “1, 4, 7, 10”. Both live in Leads › Manage, next to Connections.

A · Templates

What is wrong today: thirteen company chips wrap into three rows. Every template is a boxed card holding two more boxes, so a company’s five templates run past the bottom of the screen. The explanation is fine print full of raw codes ({{greeting}}). Save is at the very bottom. Nothing shows what the customer will actually get, and 60% of the page is empty. Every option keeps what saves today: one list of templates per company, each with a button name and its text, and the same three codes. Every option adds Insert buttons instead of typed codes, a live preview (“Hi Mark, thank you for contacting Harbor Chimney…”), drag to reorder (the order is the order of the buttons beside Send), and a Save that says when something is unsaved. Client only. The existing PUT /leads/reply-templates/{company} already saves a company’s whole list in its order.

B · Dashboard

What can be measured today (checked in the code): every lead’s arrival time, source, company and status; every message with its time; and, for a reply sent from EzBizi, who sent it, which comes from the record each reply writes. Replies sent from A1 Tools or Google’s own app have a time but no person, so they get their own row instead of being credited to anyone. Until today almost every reply went out that way: 261 of 270 up to Sep 28 (measured by Leads). So names fill in from now on. Every option opens on the last 7 days and says so when a longer range is mostly unnamed. Automatic replies (the “Thanks for contacting…” rule) are counted apart, because they would make every response time look instant. Response time = the customer’s first message to the first reply a person sent. Reply rate counts only leads that can be answered. A Google call cannot be, so it isn’t counted as unanswered. Every option has the same filters (period, company, source), is Dashboard’s tab next to Connections and Templates, and draws its graphs with the app’s own chart. Server: one new read-only endpoint, GET /leads/dashboard, for Leads to add. Option 9 also needs leads counted by hour.

7The numbers, two graphs, then everyone’s row Recommended

Six equal tiles for the period, each compared with the period before. Under them are leads per day by source, and how fast the first reply came. Then one row per person: replies sent, leads answered, their median first reply, how often they answered within 10 minutes, and how many of their leads were booked. Click a column to sort. It answers “how are we doing” and “who is doing what” on one screen.
