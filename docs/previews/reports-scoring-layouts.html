Reports › Scoring

A tab beside Performance for how each person handles calls: a card per person with AI's score, a ranking, and AI's read of their calls when you open one. Every name and number here is invented.

Built on what Performance already has per call: AI's score out of 10, the seven checks (greeted with the company name, got the name, got the address, asked what the problem is, offered a time, explained the price, asked to book), AI's "to improve" note, and Workiz's own score where Workiz scores that branch. In every option a person is ranked only after 10 scored calls in the dates chosen; below that the card says how many more are needed. Each card and detail repeats Performance's line: a guide for coaching, not a verdict.

What is real today: scoring is live since 2026-10-13 (only calls from that day on). Each scored call has a score 1–10, the seven checks, one strength and one thing to improve; who answered comes from the call log. Being built by Grok AI: a Daily and a Weekly read per person (Jose: "Daily and Weekly") with a summary, two strengths, two things to work on and the trend. Who sees it: its own permission, granted today to Administrator and Lead Manager only (Jose). Ranked within a role (Dispatcher, Remote Dispatcher) over the last 30 days, after 10 scored calls.
