Marketing · QR code stats

Jose, 2026-10-06, through the desk: "Also wonder if we can add stats to each QR Code to see how many people used it". Marketing is adding scans per day and different scanners per day. This is where each code's numbers live. It goes with the Deactivate / Reactivate change: a paused code's scans still count, so the paused stretch is shaded on its chart.

Data: each code in the list gains its last 30 days (scans, scanners, one number per day for a small chart), and GET /marketing/qr-codes/{id}/stats?days=30|90 returns every day of the period, zeros included, with the period's totals. Marketing is confirming the shape, and what one "scanner" is: per phone or per person. The word on the card follows their answer, and "people" is only used if it really counts people.

1A 30-day strip on each card, and Stats in a side panel Recommended

Each card adds a line, 128 scans · 96 people in 30 days, over a strip of 30 small bars, one per day. Stats opens a side panel: the 30 / 90-day switch, a bar chart of scans per day with scanners beside it, the totals and the busiest day, and the paused stretch shaded. Why: the card answers "is it being used?" without a click, and the panel answers "when?". The strip takes the space under the scans line, so the cards stay one size.
