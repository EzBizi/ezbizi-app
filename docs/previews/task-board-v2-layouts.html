Task Board v2: subtasks, your own columns and statuses, a board picker

Jose, 2026-10-13, on the live screen: "I feel like this can be drastically Improve, tasks should have sub tasks as well, i should be able to make custom columns, etc, please take the time to look at workiz and their UI, i should also be able to make custom status, and columns, with options wether they are text based, numerical, status, etc." His screenshots also show the organization view wrapping into four rows of about 80 board tabs, many identical ("Main · 0" ×20), because franchise names repeat across companies. Names, jobs and counts here are invented.

The same in every option. Boards are chosen from a picker grouped company › franchise, with My board first, a search, and each board's open count. There are no flat tabs, so a repeated franchise name always sits under its company. Columns are per board, of seven types: Text, Number (with a unit), Status (your own labels and colours), Date, Person, Dropdown (your own options) and Checkbox. Each can be added, renamed, moved and hidden. Task and Owner stay. Statuses are per board: the built-in Status column's labels and colours are yours to edit, and each label says whether it counts as done. Subtasks open under their task, the way Workiz's checklists sit under a job, and each has its own owner, status and due date. The task shows "2 / 5". Automations and Reports keep reading status and owner. Custom columns are offered to them by name.

1A board picker, columns added from the header, subtasks open in place RECOMMENDED

The tabs become one button: Board: Lakeview franchise ▾. It opens a searchable list, My board first, then each company with its franchises under it. A + at the end of the header row adds a column, and you pick its type. A column's ⋮ renames, edits labels or options, moves, hides or deletes it. A task's ▸ opens its subtasks under it, indented, with "+ Add subtask" last. Everything happens on the board itself, as in Monday.
