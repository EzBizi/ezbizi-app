Memberships · the page a customer joins from

The offer a customer gets after a good review, and the renewal reminder, carry a signed link to this page. It lists the business's open plans and has one Join per plan. Today it is plain HTML (resources/views/memberships/join.blade.php on session/memberships). Jose's word for the Membership program: "go with ur recommended options as usual".

Data: the page names the business by the ORGANIZATION today ("A1-Group"). A customer knows the COMPANY they booked with ("A-1 Chimney"), so the page needs the customer's own company name new. And a plan's one-line description (what a visit includes) new, optional: without it a plan shows its name and price alone. Plans, prices and the member's current year are already there.

1The frame the customer's emails already use, with a card per plan Recommended

The same band as every email a business sends through EzBizi (UI queue 39): the business's name, a teal rule under it, "via EzBizi" small, so the page looks like the message that linked to it. Each plan is a card: name, what is included, the price large (monthly plans show the month and the year), and one Join. After joining, the same page thanks them and says when the year ends and when they'll be reminded. Why: it is recognisably the same business as the text or email they just opened, and a price you can read at a glance is what decides it.
