Scheduling › Availability: earliest and suggested, with the route

Jose, 2026-10-13, via Scheduling: a zip search should show the Earliest availability and a Suggested availability, the open window nearest work already booked, so a technician can do both. He also wants "a small map with an animation of the route being drawn between the selected zip code and the other jobs it's analyzed for the suggestion". The radius is a setting. It appears in Scheduling › Availability and wherever the availability pane opens (the Leads side panel). Names, zips and jobs are invented.

The same in every option. Earliest says how far its nearest booked job is ("nearest job 23 mi away"), which is the trade-off. Suggested says why: how many jobs are within the radius, how near the nearest is, and who is already there. When the earliest day is also the best fit, one line says so instead of two cards. When nothing is within the radius in the look-ahead, there is no suggestion and a line says why. The map draws a line from the zip to each job looked at, nearest first. Jobs within the radius light up green inside the radius ring, and the rest stay grey. It plays once when the answer arrives, Replay plays it again, and with reduced motion the finished picture shows. The street map behind it is Field's (Google's, once set up); without it the map is a plain schematic. Distances are straight lines, and a note says so. With no job ever booked in that zip there is no map. Radius (default 5 mi) and look-ahead (default 14 days) are in Settings › Scheduling.

1Earliest and Suggested side by side, the map under both RECOMMENDED

The two answers sit beside each other, so the choice reads at a glance: sooner, or nearer the crew. Suggested has the stronger edge, since it is usually the one to book. The map below shows the reasoning, drawn as the answer arrives. It is 190 px tall, enough to see without pushing the windows out of view.
