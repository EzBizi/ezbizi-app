2 One card per site with its cameras inside

Each site is a card with a strip of camera stills, so you can see everything without opening a site. It shows more at a glance, but it's heavy with many sites, and it pulls a still from every camera each time the page opens.

3 A table of every camera

Every camera from every site in one table: site, camera, on/off, what it supports and status. It's quick for finding one camera, but a site going offline is just a column value, not something you'd notice.

The bridge on the PC that stays on

A small Camera bridge panel in that PC's EzBizi app (Settings › This computer). It is checked off as it goes, so whoever is standing at the PC can see where it stopped:

  1. Turn on Camera bridge (needs the app left open; start it with the PC);
  2. Enter the pairing code shown on the Cameras page;
  3. Recorder found at its address, with a box for its password. The password is kept on this PC;
  4. Cameras found, with a count;
  5. Talking to EzBizi, with the last time it did.
