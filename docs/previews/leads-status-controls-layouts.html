Leads · Status, Manage and Mark all read

From Jose, 2026-09-28, via the Leads session: "i'd like to improve this section of leads a bit, the manage, mark all leads, it all feels like it's not part of the ui and its like floating in the air. the status specially needs to be improved".

What is true today, measured:
  • Manage and Mark all read are bare text links pushed to the far right of their rows, a long empty gap away from the words and chips they act on.
  • Status in the conversation header is a borderless search field ("New" and a magnifier) alone in the corner; it opens a search list of four words. It reads as a search box.
  • Status is EzBizi's own pipeline on every source (Google, Thumbtack, chat, forms, Workiz, Messenger); nothing writes it to Google. On production 43,184 of 43,190 Google leads are still New: 3 Booked, 1 Archived, 1 Not a lead. Book already moves a lead to Booked.
In every option:
  • Nothing floats: each control sits beside the thing it acts on, inside the same box.
  • Status is never a search field. It is a coloured pill that says what it is, and changing it is one click: no pop-up, no dialog, no list to search.
  • Book stays the main action. The rest are quieter.

3Status is the inbox

Treat the list like mail, because that is how it is used: almost everything is New, so New is the Inbox, and the other three are places a lead is moved to. The status picker in the search row becomes four tabs with counts. Archive and Not a lead are icon-and-word buttons in the conversation's header, like moving a message. Sources move to a button beside Fetch new, and Mark all read sits at the head of the list it reads.

Recommendation: 1 on a desktop, 4 on a phone. It moves nothing people already know: the sources line, the chips and Book stay where they are. It fixes the three things by the rule that says why they felt loose: a control belongs beside what it acts on, in the same box. And it turns a four-word list almost nobody opens (5 changes in 43,190 leads) into the two verbs that are actually used, next to the one that already is. 2 is the most explicit about status; 3 is the biggest change, and the right one if Leads should work like an inbox.
