Financials › Home: a card per area, like Airtable's home

Jose's ask, via Financials: the Bases tab becomes a home of cards, as Airtable's is. There are six areas (Disputes & Refunds, Deposits, Repair Teams, Profit & Loss, Invoices & HQ expenses, Technician Payroll), the business's own bases, and + New base. Each card shows one live figure, where its data comes from (Native, Airtable mirror or both), and when it was last refreshed. Clicking a card opens the area: its tables as chips, and views (grid, kanban, calendar, gallery, form, split). It has to work with 1 card or 12, and for a business with no Airtable at all. Every figure here is invented.

1 A grid of equal cards, areas first, then your bases RECOMMENDED

  • The grid: equal-size cards, four across on a wide screen. Each has a coloured icon, the name, one figure in bold, the source badge, and "refreshed 4 min ago".
  • "Your areas" comes first, then "Your bases" ending in a dashed + New base card.
  • With no Airtable: the badges simply read Native. Nothing mentions Airtable.
  • A search box appears from 8 cards up.
