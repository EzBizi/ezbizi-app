Project Management overhaul: one frame, one flow, and a Rules tab

Jose (2026-10-23): "impossible to understand … i don't know where to do stuff, i don't know what is going on, or how things are measured, all of the screens have no correlation … make an extra tab with the rules of what triggers what changes … look at all the screens, do an overhaul."

I reviewed every tab. The main problems:

  • Each tab has its own header, filters and colours.
  • Nothing says how a number is counted.
  • Several numbers contradict each other. For example, "Payments stopped 1,280" opens a list of a different size.
  • The payment rule ("Action 1") is switched off in production. It skipped all 338 jobs this week.

Every name here is invented.

1 One frame on every tab; the stages read as a flow; every number explains itself RECOMMENDED

The frame: the same on all seven tabs

  • Top to bottom:
    1. the tab strip;
    2. one sentence saying what the tab is for, plus "How this is measured ›", which opens the Rules tab at the right place;
    3. the scope bar: which jobs are counted, at which place, with Change;
    4. the filter bar: search, place, then that tab's own filters, always in this order.
  • Stages: the five stages always appear in the same order, as a flow: Waiting review → Ready to schedule → Scheduled → In progress → Done, waiting payment. Each has one colour, used only for that stage.
  • Colour rules: red means only "needs attention now". Money appears only for people who can see it.
  • Explanations: hovering, or tapping ⓘ, on any number shows the rule that produced it, for example "Pending jobs with a deposit paid and money still due".

Tabs, each with one job

  • Overview (was Dashboard): what needs attention today. The flow of five stages with counts, then the "Needs attention" list with three cards (paid but not started past 10 days · payments stopped · waiting review with no reason), then by place and by technician.
  • Jobs: one list. The flow on top doubles as the stage filter, and every number on Overview opens this tab filtered to exactly that number.
  • Assignments: who does what. Payment-rule cards grouped by technician, drag to assign.
  • Repair team (Reports and Table merged): the weekly report, with a Cards / Table switch, from Financials' sheet.
  • Rules (new): how it all works, built from live settings and automations.
  • Logs: what EzBizi changed, and why.
  • Settings: change the rules. Each card links back to its line on the Rules tab.

The Rules tab: what triggers what, read from your live settings

  • Read-only, generated from live settings and automation rules, so it can't fall out of date. Each block has "Change ›", which opens the right Settings card or the rule in Automations.
  • Which jobs count: the scope, where it's set, and warnings ("Done pending approval is counted but belongs to no stage").
  • How a job moves between stages: one line per stage (its statuses plus the schedule and money rules), with today's count.
  • What changes jobs automatically: each rule as When → Only if → Then, with on/off, runs and skips this week, and the last reason it skipped.
  • Daily checks: the 10-day email, payments stopped and the estimate clock: when they run, what they look at and who they tell.
  • How numbers are measured: every tile and chart in plain words.

2 Keep today's seven tabs, just add the frame and the Rules tab

The same tabs (Dashboard, Reports, Table, Jobs, Assignments, Logs, Settings), with the common frame, the ⓘ explanations and the new Rules tab, but no merging and no flow. It's a smaller change, but Reports and Table still feel separate and the Dashboard stays a grid of tiles.

3 A guided home: the flow, with everything else behind it

The first screen is just the stage flow and Needs attention. Every other tab moves under a "More" menu. It's the simplest view day to day, but setup and reports are a click further away.
