Work Hours › Paid Time Off › Requests

Jose's new feature, via HR: staff request their own paid time off and approvers decide.

  • A request for more days than are left is refused, unless the place allows going negative.
  • Approving puts the days into "Used".
  • Nobody decides their own request.
  • Approvers are notified of each new request, and the requester of the decision.

Every name and date here is invented.

1 A Requests view inside Paid Time Off: "Waiting for you" on top, your own requests below RECOMMENDED

  • Where it lives: Paid Time Off gets a segment, Balances · Requests, so it isn't a new tab. A count of requests waiting for you appears on Requests, and the notification opens it.
  • Waiting for you is for approvers only. Each row shows the person, the dates (consecutive days grouped, ½ marked), the total, their note, and "Approving leaves N". The buttons are Approve and Deny; Deny asks for the reason on the same row, with no pop-up.
  • Request time off opens a box under it, with no pop-up. It has:
    • the same calendar as Record days off: tap a day for a full day, again for a half, a third time to clear, with already-taken days tinted;
    • your balance before and after;
    • a note;
    • Send request.
    If it's more than you have left, it says so in one sentence and Send stays off (unless your place allows going negative).
  • My requests: each one with a status chip (Waiting, Approved by X, Denied by X with the reason, Cancelled), and Cancel while it's still waiting.
  • Search and filters: search plus a status filter for everything else.

2 A separate "Requests" tab in Work Hours

The same content, but as its own tab beside Paid Time Off and Daily approval. It's easier to find for approvers, but it adds another tab, and staff look for their balance and their requests in two places.

3 Requests inside each person's panel

Requests show on each person's balance panel, and approvers review them person by person. It keeps everything together per person, but an approver has to open each person to find what's waiting.
