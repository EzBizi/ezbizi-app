▶ How-to video: plays in the app everywhere, with fullscreen and download

Jose, today: "video should be seen on desktop, mobile, etc, right not it only works on web, secondly the fullscreen option and also the download option all inside the app." On Windows the panel only offers "Open the video" today, because the player has no Windows half. That's fixed with the player's own Windows implementation, so the video plays in the panel on Windows, macOS, Android, iOS and web, and never opens a browser. Fullscreen and Download are new; chapters still jump to their time.

Download saves the file: a Save dialog on Windows and macOS, the browser's download on web, and the phone's gallery on Android and iOS (the phone asks once whether EzBizi may save to Photos). Fullscreen fills the screen: the window goes fullscreen on a computer, and the phone turns sideways. Esc or ✕ comes back to the panel, at the same point in the video.
