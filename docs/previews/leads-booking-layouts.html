The decision this screen really makes: the two mistakes are not equally bad customer_matches is up to three customers whose last ten digits match the lead's number, and the code calls it “advisory only”. So the panel has to decide what to do when a match exists, and there are two ways to be wrong:
MistakeWhat it costsIs it noticed?
Attach to the wrong existing customer a stranger's job lands in somebody's history, and the two people are now one record No. It looks exactly like a correct booking
Create a duplicate customer two Jane Does, history split until somebody merges them Yes. Two identical rows in the customer list
A shared line, a family, a landlord and a tenant all produce a genuine phone match between different people. So neither outcome may be the silent default. Which is why every layout below ASKS when a match exists, and only then. No match: straight to a new customer, no question, because there is nothing to decide. One or more matches: the choice is required before Book will go, with “Create a new customer” sitting as a peer of the matches rather than as the fallback. One extra decision, taken only at the moment somebody is actually best placed to make it.
Two things the panel must not imply The customer fields here do not edit the lead. The brief is explicit that the lead stays evidence — what Google sent, unaltered. So the panel is prefilled FROM the lead and typing in it changes the customer being created, not the lead. That is a same-looking-data, different-owner trap, and every layout says it once in the panel rather than leaving it to be discovered. And a time here is not an appointment. scheduled_for is a field on the job — the same one the New Job panel takes. Scheduling has no appointment model, so nothing is reserved, no slot is consumed and no one is notified. The layouts label it When the job is scheduled for rather than “Book a time”, because a screen that says Book twice about two different things will be read as having made a booking twice.

The house shapes are FranchiseField, the tenant's own jobs.statuses setting — never a hardcoded list — and CalendarDayField, which is typed boxes with an inline calendar rather than a dialog, because this app has no modals. A lead at company level has no franchise, so it is a required pick. A placed lead prefills it. That is the one field that can be neither guessed nor skipped.
