Read from usage_pane.dart “What Twilio charged for this phone account, by day and by kind. Collected each night.” Read-only — nothing here is configured — so under the approved pane pattern the footer is null, and the retry stays in the head because its error is a failed load. Existing states kept: “Nothing collected yet. The nightly import fills this in from Twilio.” and “EzBizi could not read the cost figures the server sent.”
Two findings, and the second one is a question for you rather than a design 1 · “Collected each night” means today is missing, and nothing on screen says so. The figures are an overnight import, so the most recent day shown is yesterday and today's calls are not in any total. Somebody checking spend after a heavy morning sees a number that is silently a day behind — the same shape as attestation saying “not captured” and Scheduling saying “configured capacity”. Every layout states the collection time and marks today as not-yet-counted rather than as zero. 2 · Seeing costs requires permission to CHANGE phone settings. The refusal says it outright: “You can see what the phone lines do, but not what they cost. That needs permission to change phone settings.” So a bookkeeper who should read the bill and touch nothing cannot, and anybody who can read it can also reconfigure the phone system. That is a server-side permission decision and not mine to change — I am flagging it because this is the screen where it bites. If it should be its own permission, that is a Phone-module change and worth saying now rather than after the layout ships.
