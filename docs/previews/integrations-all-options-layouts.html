Jose, 2026-10-13: Integrations shows only what is connected. He wants every option shown, with the ones not connected connectable right there, so he can connect them all. Workiz and Airtable also show as grey squares. Today an unconnected provider is a plain "Also available" chip (Social) or is not shown at all (Advertising, Mailing). The counts, and which cards say "not available" or "ask an administrator", are examples only.

The same in every option. Each of the four sections lists every provider EzBizi supports: Mailing (Mailchimp), Social (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, X, Bluesky), Advertising and listings (Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Thumbtack) and Reporting (Workiz, Airtable). Every card wears its logo; Workiz's and Airtable's are already in the app and were simply never wired. Connect sits on the card and connects in the current view, as the provider's page does today. A Connect button is only drawn where it can work. Where EzBizi can't connect a provider yet (the server's own "not ready" reason), the card says so instead. Where you lack the permission, it says "Ask an administrator". Clicking a card still opens its page, with the accounts and whose they are.

Connected cards come first and look as they do now. The rest follow in the same grid with a dashed edge, one line about what the provider is for, and a Connect button. Nothing is hidden behind a chip, and you can see at a glance which cards are yours, because only those are solid.
