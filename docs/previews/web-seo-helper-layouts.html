Web Management · SEO Helper

Asked by Web Management, from Jose, 2026-09-28: "add it, see if you can improve it as well … a SEO Helper basically". It replaces A1's per-page editor for each page's Google title and description. The websites keep their current SEO plugin as the renderer; the Helper edits and analyses and stores nothing of its own. The plugin is never named on screen.

What is fixed in every option
  • Where: its own section in the SEO strip, General · Social links · Pages · Cities · Posts · Templates · SEO Helper, for the website chosen in the rail. Everything opens inside that pane: nothing goes full screen (Jose's containment rule).
  • Three levels: the site's health (counts that each open the pages behind them, plus site-wide warnings such as a title template still saying "UPDATED 2024"), the page list with one traffic light per page, and the page editor.
  • The editor: a Google result preview (desktop and phone; the title cut by pixel width at about 600 px, the description at about 155 characters, as Google does), the focus keyphrase, the SEO title and meta description each with a live length meter, and insert chips in words (Page title · Separator · Site name · Year) for the variables; the preview shows the result. Analysis in two groups, SEO and Readability, each check good / ok / bad with one sentence saying what to do, re-checked as you type (a draft is analysed without saving). A social preview, an Advanced fold (hide from Google, canonical URL), and Save.
  • Two things it does better, made visible: the analysis reads the live page, what Google sees, so page-builder pages are judged on their real words; and the site-wide duplicates: two pages chasing one keyphrase are named on both.
  • Viewing needs SEO access; saving needs edit access, and without it the fields are read-only and Save is not drawn.

1Health on top, the list under it, the editor in its place recommended

One column that reads top to bottom. The health card is first because it is the reason to open the section: what is wrong across the site, in counts that each filter the list below. Choosing a page replaces the list with its editor, with a back arrow, inside the same pane. The editor is two columns: what you type on the left, what Google shows and what the checks say on the right.
