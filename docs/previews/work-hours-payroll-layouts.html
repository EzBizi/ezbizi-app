It's one week (by default the week yesterday fell in) for the people in the top-bar view: hours, rate, bonus, deductions and tech expenses add up to weekly pay. A manager approves each person, or approves everyone at once, and downloads the .xlsx. Hours over 40 are shown but not paid. Editing bonus, deductions or the note un-approves the person. The layout matches Daily approval (136).

1 · Table, edit in the row (recommended)

This is Daily approval's frame. The top has the week stepper, the view name, chips (People, Hours, Pay, Waiting, With warnings), a search bar, Approve all and Download .xlsx. Bonus and deductions are fields right in the row and save when you leave the field. The note is a pencil that opens a single line under the row. Tech expenses has a ▸ that opens the lines (job, item, qty, amount, paid). Warnings show as chips, and the total sits at the foot.
