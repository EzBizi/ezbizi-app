Jobs · what Workiz knows about a job

From Jobs: Workiz v2's own data is now on every job (Jose, 2026-10-06: "take advantage of everything v2 has to offer"). The job panel today shows Workiz's money as one card (queue 31), then the job's fields, lines, materials and inspections. These options are where the rest goes.

Data: all there now, read only. For anyone who can open a job: Workiz's fields in Workiz's own names (71% of recent jobs have some), and photos and files, including inspection-report PDFs (18% of jobs have them, about 8 each). With Jobs Financials only: invoices (total, due, tax, tip, dates), payments (date, amount, charge or refund, who collected), Workiz's estimates (with Workiz's own status: Won, Pending, Declined…), timesheets (rare), and the "Pricing" field. Without Financials none of these appear, not even as an empty space. These are Workiz's own records, not EzBizi Estimates or invoices, and every one says so.

1Tabs in the job panel: Job, Money, Photos & files Recommended

The panel gets three tabs, each with its count. Job is today's panel, with Workiz's fields as a two-column fact list right under the status: what the job is (System type, Real wood, Time frame) before anything to edit. Money (Financials only) is today's money card, then what makes it up: the invoice, the payments as a dated line with who collected each, Workiz's estimates with their status, and time on site. Photos & files is a grid of photos with the PDFs listed under it; a photo opens large in place. Why: a job is already a long panel. Tabs keep each question one tap away (what is it? is it paid? what did they find?) instead of somewhere down one scroll.
