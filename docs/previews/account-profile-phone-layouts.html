Your account — the phone version

The premise, and it is Jose's correction rather than mine Choosing layout 3 for the desktop does not mean the phone gets layout 3 narrowed. A phone version can be a completely different design for a different user in a different situation — and every “at 375px” I have drawn until now has been a desktop layout squeezed, which is a habit rather than a decision. So this is not derived from layout 3. Desktop layout 3 stays exactly as approved.
What a phone user actually does here, which is not what a desktop user does They came to change ONE thing. A photo, a phone number, turn on two-factor, change a password after a prompt. Nobody opens their account page on a phone to read their role and the ten places they can act on. So the desktop question and the phone question are different. Desktop answers “show me everything about my account” — which is why a two-column layout with the facts always visible is right there. A phone should answer “what do you want to change?”, and a long scrolling form answers that badly: you scroll past nine things you did not come for to reach the one you did. The read-only facts drop to the bottom, or behind a tap. On desktop they cost nothing because they sit in space that was empty anyway. On a phone every one of them is something between you and the thing you opened the app to do.
One thing that is a phone problem and does not exist on desktop A form with one Save at the bottom is hostile on a phone. The keyboard covers roughly half the screen, so a footer at the end of a scroll is behind the keyboard exactly while you are typing — and “two changes not saved” is invisible at the moment it matters most. Layouts 1 and 2 avoid it entirely by making each screen do one thing, so Save is always in the bar and always reachable. Layout 3 keeps a single form and has to pin its footer above the keyboard, which is possible and is one more thing to get right.

Pick a number. 1 is my recommendation. 4 shows what a detail screen looks like, which is the same in 1 and 2.
