Phone › Settings panes — reviewed, then redesigned

What I read before drawing anything All 11,243 lines of client/lib/screens/phone/ — every pane, both settings tabs, and the shared widgets they import. The rail itself is already approved as layout 1 of phone-settings-rail-layouts.html and Trust is approved and being built, so neither is re-proposed here. This is about what happens inside a section once you click it.
One real defect, found by reading rather than by looking Four of twelve panes show an error and offer no way out of it. porting, ring_groups, voicemail and number_quality render ErrorNote with no retry control — so a failed load is a dead end, and the only recovery is to navigate to another section and back. The other eight all offer “Try again”. This is not a design preference; it is eight panes agreeing and four disagreeing, and the four are wrong. CORRECTED, by the Phone session, while building it: “every pane gets a retry in the head” is too broad and would have destroyed work. The head's retry RELOADS. A FORM's error is usually a refused SAVE, so offering a reload there throws away what somebody just typed in the course of offering to recover it — retrying a refused save is pressing Save again. The rule is: a pane whose error is a failed LOAD gets a retry in the head; a refused SAVE is reported at the footer and retried with Save. Most panes can do both, so most need both treatments and must tell the two errors apart.
Three findings I had to correct on myself, because the first version of each was wrong 1 · “No shared skeleton” — false. widgets/async_body.dart exists and is well documented. What is true is narrower and still worth fixing: 0 of 13 settings panes use it, while all three tabs do. Thirteen hand-rolled copies of loading / failed / loaded. 2 · “So the bug AsyncBody prevents is live” — also false. Its docblock warns that setting the flag on every fetch makes “saving one field tear the whole page down to a spinner”. I checked all thirteen: none sets _loading inside a save. They duplicate the pattern and implement it correctly. It is a consistency cost and thirteen places to change, not a live defect — and saying otherwise would have been the more dramatic and less true report. 3 · “number_quality has no loading state” — false. It uses _looking, and my grep looked for _loading. A measurement whose instrument was narrower than the claim, which is the thing this codebase has spent all day cataloguing.

Thirteen panes, four shapes. Rather than thirteen unrelated drawings, the audit below sorts every pane into the shape it actually is; layouts 4–6 draw the pattern carrying each shape. 1 is the recommended pattern. 2 and 3 are the alternatives it beat. 7 is 375px.
