The play control is deliverable, and I checked before drawing one. client/lib/screens/phone/recording_player.dart already exists and takes bytes, not a URL — the client fetches the authenticated endpoint itself and hands the bytes to a blob. A wire value is on screen today, and the fix arrived with this brief The list currently reads +1 (513) 555-0100 · xcat:service area business home inspector · 22h ago. xcat: is Google's category identifier leaking to a customer's screen. service is a better source — but it is an identifier too: fireplace_chimney. I had drawn “Chimney sweep”, which was an assumption wearing a field's clothes. It needs humanising on the client — underscores to spaces, first letter up — fireplace_chimney → Fireplace chimney. And that humaniser has a ceiling worth naming before it is built. Mechanical sentence-case is right for fireplace_chimney and confidently wrong on any acronym: hvac_repair becomes “Hvac repair”, tv_mounting becomes “Tv mounting”. That is worse than showing the raw identifier — an identifier reads as a system value and people ignore it, while “Hvac” reads as the business having misspelled its own trade, in front of the customer whose trade it is. Measured across 23,875 live leads, so this is settled with data. service has exactly two states: fireplace_chimney (22,299) and absent (1,576). No acronym anywhere, so the mechanical rule ships as written and the ceiling above goes in a comment rather than into code. A second tenant in another trade is the first real test of it, and their real list is when to add a map. And the absent case is 1,576 leads, which I had not drawn at all. Six per cent of them carry no service. The header omits the segment rather than saying “No service given”, and that is a departure from the Your account rule that an empty value must say so. The discriminator is whether anybody can close the gap: on Your account a blank phone number is something YOU can fill in, so silence looks like a failed load. Here it is Google's data, nobody can supply it, and a row of “not given” is a line of type that reports a fact no reader can act on. One more reason the category never goes on screen: it is WRONG. 23,686 of those leads are classified xcat:service_area_business_home_inspector — Google calling a chimney company a home inspector, and 12 more calling it a landscaper. So the line on screen today is not merely a wire value leaking; it is a false statement about the business, shown to that business. Same rule applies to the channel. last_contact_channel is Google's word — PHONE_CALL, MESSAGE, SMS, WHATSAPP, EMAIL, BOOKING. None of those go on screen as written. a1leads shows LS Ads and LS Text, which are its own shorthand and mean nothing here; every layout below says Call and Text.
Two of the three filters have nothing behind them a1leads has Unread, Unreplied and Starred. Only Unreplied has data — last_contact_from = CONSUMER, meaning they spoke last. Unread needs a per-user read marker and Starred needs a flag; neither exists. So they are not drawn, and that is the recommendation. A filter that is present and always empty is worse than an absent one: it teaches people the list is broken. They are shown struck through in layout 1 only, so the choice is visible rather than silent.
