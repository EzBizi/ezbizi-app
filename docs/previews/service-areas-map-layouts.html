Scheduling › Service areas: draw them on a map

Jose (2026-10-21): areas are drawn rather than typed as ZIP lists. Drawing a shape works out which ZIPs it covers. Before anything is saved you see what would be added, what belongs to another area (and whether to take it), and what would drop out. Deleting an area asks where its ZIPs go, which fixes the bug Jose hit. Every name here is invented.

1 The map with every area, a draw tool, and a review before saving RECOMMENDED

  • The map: every area as a coloured shape (a colour per branch in the company view, per area in a branch view), with ZIP dots.
  • On the left: the area list with ZIP counts.
  • Editing an area: pick it, draw or reshape its outline, and see the review on the right:
    • Adds ZIPs, in green.
    • Belong to another area, in orange, each with a "Take it" tick. You can only take from areas you're allowed to edit.
    • Would drop out, in red.
    • Typed ZIPs outside the drawing, which are kept.
  • Save applies only what you ticked. "Clear drawing" removes the shape and keeps the typed ZIPs. Adding or removing ZIPs by hand still works.

Deleting an area opens a side pane (never a dialog): "Downtown has 18 ZIPs. Where should they go?" The choices are Move them to [area ▾] or Leave them unassigned, then Delete. Afterwards it says "18 moved to East" or "18 now unassigned".

2 One area at a time, the map inside its page

The area list stays a list. Opening an area shows its own map, with only that area's shape and its neighbours greyed out. It's simpler, but you don't see all areas together, which is where overlaps show up.

3 Keep ZIP lists; the map is view-only

Areas stay as typed ZIP lists, with a map that only shows them. It's the smallest change, but nobody can draw, which is what Jose asked for.
