Re-Scheduling › open spots, before and after the move

Jose, today: "perhaps on the reschedule screen on that blank spot on the right bottom, we can include the scheduling before and after for the area too, as in the amount of spots before and after". The space is under Now → Suggested and Move. Moving a job frees a spot on its current day and takes one on the suggested day, so each day shows its open spots before → after, window by window. This folds into the day plan going out in 0.0.0429 rather than adding a block. Every name and number here is invented.

Scheduling adds the current day's windows to the suggestion (capacity, taken and open per window, and which one the job is in), so both days come from the server. Open spots are the area's own numbers, as Availability shows them.
