The Jobs session's ask, 2026-09-29: Jose approved estimates as EzBizi records living in Jobs. Where they live, and the editor with lines. Most of A1's 2,388 have a total and no lines.

Every option needs estimates.view and the Financials toggle; the job and the customer come from the same franchise, and lines can change only while an estimate is a draft or sent.

Jobs gets two tabs, Jobs and Estimates. The estimates list is the jobs list's twin: status chips with counts, what is quoted and accepted, and the estimate docked beside it. The editor is the lines as a small table you type into, with the total under them and the next step at the foot.
