Communication · Settings · General and Call flow

Jose, 2026-10-05, on Call flow: "I feel we can also improve this screen on communication - settings - call flow", and on General: "Same for this one". They are drawn together because they are one subject: General sets how the company answers a call, and Call flow shows what each line then does.

General todayFive pickers stretched across the whole screen, three switches and one Save at the bottom. Ring group reads "Nobody — every call goes to voicemail" in grey. Nothing on it says that this one setting is why seven lines ring nobody.
Call flow todayOne card per line. Seven of eight cards repeat the same four steps word for word, each with a sentence about Twilio and the raw number (+12395550142). "Transcribed" and "Recorded" are drawn like buttons and do nothing. The red "Nobody answers this line" has no button to fix it.

1One screen: the company's answer drawn once and changed in place, the lines under it Recommended

General and Call flow become one screen called Call flow. The top is Harbor Chimney's answer, left to right in the order a call meets it. Click a box to choose another value (the same search picker General uses). It saves on the spot, then the boxes re-read what the phone system will actually do, so the screen never shows what you typed in place of what will happen. A wrong box is red and carries its fix: Pick who rings turns all seven lines on at once. Under it, one row per line says only whether that line follows the company or has its own answer. Opening a line shows its chart (today's Call flow chart) with a ← back, so the chart's separate entry goes too. Why: the answer appears once instead of eight times, the change that makes seven lines ring is on the box that is wrong, and Settings loses two entries and the duplicate heading.

Data: one addition from Communication: in GET /phone/call-flow, each step says where its value comes from, from: company | line new, so "follows Harbor Chimney" and "its own" are the server's words, not a guess. The pickers write the same default_* fields General writes today.
