Six numbered layouts: 1–3 desktop, 4–6 mobile. Every one is drawn light and dark side by side, because the finding below is that they are not the same design.

Measured first: primary INVERTS between light and dark Probed against EzBiziTheme.light() and .dark(), not picked by eye:
primarytext on itcontrast
Light#00708f dark teal white5.65:1
Dark#00aeef bright cyan near-black7.94:1
So “fill our bubbles with primary and put light text on them” is right in light mode and upside down in dark — there it is a bright cyan slab with black text. Any colour direction stated as one sentence is only half designed, which is why all six are drawn twice. And the colour a1leads uses is already taken here. Their business messages are saturated red. In this app red is error — #ba1a1a light, #ffb4ab dark — and errorContainer plus a count is the settled vocabulary for “this is broken and nobody noticed”, which Phone shipped four instances of in one day. Colouring our own messages red would make every message the business sent look like a fault. That is the one thing from a1leads that cannot be copied, and it is not visible from looking at the two apps side by side.
Every pairing below passes AA in both brightnesses — measured, not assumed onPrimaryContainer on primaryContainer 7.25:1 (both), onPrimary on primary 5.65 light / 7.94 dark, onSecondaryContainer on secondaryContainer 7.23:1 (both), primary as text on surfaceContainerLow 5.12 / 6.78. The floor is 4.5:1 for body text. One token is off limits without a decision: tertiaryContainer. Material generates it from the cyan seed as #e8ddff, a pale lavender. Nothing else in this app is purple, so any layout wanting a third hue is proposing to introduce one — layout 2 is the only one that does, and it uses secondaryContainer instead for exactly that reason.
