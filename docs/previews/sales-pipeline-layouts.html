Sales — pipeline tracker

New top-level tab Sales. Every job in Done pending approval with a quote becomes a deal automatically (name, phone, email, state, job #, quote, lead date come from the job). The salesperson only sets Stage, Urgency, Next follow-up and adds notes — replacing Jay's / Jason's spreadsheets. Stages from the sheet: Follow up · Call back · Sold · Ghosted · Disqualified. Urgency: ASAP · Next week · Next month · 60+ days. Pick one desktop (D1–D3) and one mobile (M1–M3).
