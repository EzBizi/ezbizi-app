AI Assistance › Staff bot: one number, a menu for each role

Jose asked for one WhatsApp number whose menu changes with the person's role. Everything it says is kept in EzBizi, where it can be edited. The Office and Technician menus below are Jose's list. Every person, phone number and figure here is invented.

Linked, never copied. Most items point at a Training guide, down to its chapter and section. Editing the guide changes what the bot says, and nothing is kept twice. Where nothing in EzBizi covers an item yet (Monday training, the Before & After gallery, the trainers' videos, the Google reviews link, a stand-alone dispatch speech), the item becomes a Library entry written in place: text, a link, a video or photos. One item, Daily Conversion Updates, is made from Reports each day. The bot knows who is writing from their phone number on their EzBizi profile, and that person's role decides the menu they get.

Who builds what: Training keeps the guides; Meta runs the WhatsApp number and sends the menus; Grok AI writes the free answers over them; UI/UX builds this editor. WhatsApp's limits shape the editor (Meta, via Grok AI): a list holds 10 rows and one is the bot's own Find a location, so a menu takes 9 topics; a row's title is 24 characters and its line 72; an answer has at most 3 buttons; media goes one per message (photos 5 MB, video 16 MB), so a gallery is 3–5 photos or a link to a gallery page, and long training videos go as links. A message the bot sends unasked, such as a morning Daily Conversion Update, needs a template Meta approves; asked for from the menu, it needs none. A typed question gets an AI answer from the role's guide and the Library, so the menu is a shortcut, not the only way in. The counters below show how much is left.
