Its own copy already draws HR’s line: “Newsletters and anything else your own people write go out from this address. Signing in, password resets and joining messages always come from EzBizi, whatever is set here.”

So this is not a new four-state panel. B is a STATE the card gains — putting it anywhere else gives one fact two voices that can disagree. A is the gap, and the three options differ on where it goes, because “EzBizi is not delivering anything” is not a setting and does not belong in a settings card about your own address. Nothing here shows missing — that is AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID and belongs to staff.
