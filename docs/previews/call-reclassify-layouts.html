Calls: set New/Existing and Booked/Not booked by hand

Jose, 11-03, for Trey: "reclassify from existing to new and viceverse as well as to booke from not booked and viceversa". This applies in the call details on Performance › Calls, on a person's calls, and on Scoring's calls. Only people with reports.handles see the controls; everyone else sees the ✎ mark. Every name here is invented.

1 Two segmented controls in the details RECOMMENDED

  • Client New | Existing and Booking Booked | Not booked each sit on one line in the call details. One tap sets the value. "Convert to booked" becomes the Booked segment.
  • Once a value is set by hand, "Set by Trey · Automatic ↺" appears beside it. Automatic undoes it, back to what the phones and Workiz say.
  • The row's pill carries a small ✎. Hovering it shows "Set by Trey Brannan".

2 Tappable pills

Tap the Client or Booking pill on the row to open a small inline menu with the other value and "Back to automatic". It's fast and needs no details pane, but nothing shows it's tappable until you try, and it's easy to hit by mistake while scrolling.

3 A folded "Correct this call" section

The details get a folded "Correct this call ▸" section. Opening it shows both fields with Save and Undo. It's the safest against stray clicks, but it takes two extra taps for something Trey does many times a day.
