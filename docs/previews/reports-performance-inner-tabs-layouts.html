Reports › Performance · the tabs inside it

Jose 2026-10-09: "I feel like this performance screen is a mess, specially the inner tabs." Today on a wide screen each call is a line of small text with its own empty audio bar stretched across the whole page and the talk time far away at the right edge. The caller's line name repeats ("A1 - New Client · A1 - New Client"). Calls has up to three rows of chips (All / Missed / Existing / Not booked / New, then New/Existing client, then Missed call / Voicemail / Blocked with a paragraph), counted two ways ("Missed 3", "Voicemail (3)"). People opens a grey box that mixes a setting ("Answers for") with the calls. Every name, number and line below is invented.

In every option: a call is one row: time, who took it, the caller with the first words they said, small tags for the line and source (once, never repeated), a client badge (New / Existing) and a result badge (Booked, Not booked, Voicemail, Missed, Blocked), talk time, and one ▶ button. The audio bar and the whole transcript open under that row only when you press ▶ or the words. Calls get one filter bar with two choices, Result and Client, each counted the same way. Why a call reached nobody is the row's badge, and its explanation moves into an ⓘ. Not booked keeps Convert to booked on the row. Existing calls keep the AI's reason as a badge, with Claude/Grok as a small switch at the end of the bar. On People, "Answers for" moves to the top of the opened row, apart from the calls. No figures change and nothing new is needed from the server.

1Same three tabs, every call one tidy row Recommended

Overview, People and Calls stay. Calls becomes a table with the filter bar above. Overview's figures become white tiles you click to open Calls already filtered. A person's row opens their calls as the same rows. Why: it fixes what's messy (the stretched bars, the three chip rows, the repeated names) without moving anything you already know, and the same row reads the same on all three tabs.
