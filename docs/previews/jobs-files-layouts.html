Jobs › Photos & files: add, see and remove a job's own

Slice (b) of moving off Workiz: a job keeps its own photos and files in EzBizi. Workiz's stay beside them, read-only, until they are copied in, which waits on your storage decision. Every name here is invented.

In every option:

  • You add by dragging files in, choosing files, or, on a phone, Take a photo.
  • A photo opens full size in the panel. A file opens or downloads.
  • Remove asks once.
  • Each item shows who added it and when.
  • Workiz's items have no Remove.

1 The job's own on top with a drop zone, Workiz's underneath RECOMMENDED

  • "Added in EzBizi" is a grid of thumbnails, with a drop zone and Add photos or files. Hover a thumbnail for ✕ Remove.
  • "From Workiz · read-only" sits under it, as today, with a note that they'll be copied in later.
  • Uploads show progress on their own tile. A failed upload says why, with Try again.
