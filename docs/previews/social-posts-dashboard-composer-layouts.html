Social posts · a dashboard and a full composer

Jose, 2026-10-12: "Now that we got the META Approval i believe we ought to improve this a bit and divide posts between a Dashboard that shows all of the data and a full fledged composer." Today one screen does both: the composer on top, the tiles and the twenty newest posts under it, Calendar behind a second chip. The failures read as raw network errors in red.

What a dashboard can show without saying something untrue. Every number here is one EzBizi already holds: the posts it sent, each account's answer, what is scheduled, and which accounts can post. No likes, reach or follower counts: the Meta review told Meta, for pages_read_engagement and Instagram, that "EzBizi does not read followers, comments or analytics" (docs/meta-app-review-submission.md). Reading them needs a new App Review first, so they are left out, not drawn as empty boxes.

Same in every option: the Newsletter's shape (Dashboard first, then the composer: Jose's rule that landing in an empty composer answers no question); the server's tiles (Scheduled / Sent / Failed, counted over every post); a failure's reason under it (two lines at most; More shows the whole of the network's answer) with Retry beside it; View post wherever the network gave a link; Calendar kept as it is. The composer keeps every field and rule it has now (per-network character limits, TikTok's questions, Now / Later, one picture or video) and gets the room: a big writing box, a drop zone for the file, a large preview, and Publish fixed at the bottom. Names, posts and numbers are invented.

1Dashboard · Composer · Calendar under Posts, like the Newsletter RECOMMENDED

The Compose / Calendar chips become three: Dashboard (where Posts opens), Composer, Calendar. The dashboard works as a to-do list: what failed and how to fix it, then what goes out next, then every post. New post opens the Composer. A failed post's Retry and a scheduled post's row work in place, so you only go to the Composer to write.

2One row fewer: Dashboard and Compose sit beside Accounts

Posts' own row goes. Social Media Management's tabs become Dashboard · Compose · Calendar · Accounts · QR Codes, three rows of tabs down to two. The dashboard is one timeline: what is coming, then each day's posts, with every account's answer as a coloured dot. The composer shows every ticked network's preview at once instead of one at a time.

Cost: each tab is grantable on its own (marketing.posts.view today). Splitting Posts into Dashboard and Compose means two new permission keys, and the old "posts" tick must stay grantable. That is a server change in Marketing and a permission split, which has gone wrong twice before. Option 1 needs neither: all three panes sit under the Posts grant.

3Dashboard by account, composer with the accounts down the side

Same three panes as option 1. The dashboard leads with one card per account: whether it can post, what it sent and what failed, and its last post. Then the list of every post, filtered by account. The composer puts the accounts in a column on the left, so each one's limit and note sits next to its tick.

Cost: the per-account numbers ("9 sent · 2 failed") are a new count on the server (Marketing). The list carries only the twenty newest posts, and counting those would understate any busy account. The column of accounts is also narrower writing space than option 1 on a laptop.
