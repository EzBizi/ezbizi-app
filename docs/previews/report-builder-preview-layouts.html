Jose 2026-10-09: "i also want a preview view, as you select the items and be able to order them too". Today the builder is a list of modules on the left and, on the right, a name box, a "Columns" list of picked fields with no way to reorder them, filters as plain text rows, and Save. You only see what the report looks like after saving it. Every name and number below is invented.

In every option: a live preview of the first 10 real rows, which fills in as each field is ticked, with the column just added tinted for a moment. Columns drag to reorder, and each has ✕ to remove it. Fields are searchable, grouped by module, and show their type (date, money, text). Around the preview there's room for Filters (field, condition, value), Sort, Group by (with a subtotal per group) and Totals (sum or count on money and number columns), plus the date range and Save. The list of what Reports can offer (in progress, Workiz's builder as the yardstick) only adds choices to these menus. It doesn't change the layout.

1Fields on the left, preview on the right, columns as chips you drag Recommended

Tick fields on the left, and each one joins the column bar and the preview at once. Drag a chip in the bar to reorder, or drag the preview's header itself. Filters, Sort, Group by and Totals are one row of buttons above the preview, each showing what it's set to. Why: you pick, arrange and check in one screen, and the preview takes most of the room because it's the report.

2The preview is the builder: add and arrange columns on the table itself

There's no separate column list. "＋ Add column" sits at the end of the header and opens the searchable field list. You drag a header to move it. Clicking a header opens its menu: sort, group by, total, filter on it, remove. Why: the most direct, like a spreadsheet. But filters and grouping are hidden in header menus until you know to look, and a new user faces an empty table with one button.

3Four steps, with the preview beside every step

1 What to report on (Jobs, Customers…). 2 Columns: pick them, then order them in a list with drag handles. 3 Filters, sort, group and totals. 4 Name and save. The preview stays on the right throughout. Why: the easiest to follow for someone who has never built a report. But it's slower for someone who has, and going back a step to add one column is a detour.
