Empty screens · one way to say "nothing here yet"

From the full UI review (2026-10-08). Empty screens are drawn about six ways: centred or top-left, in a card or loose. Many name the next step as plain text ("Add one in Settings", "Settings › Integrations", "the Library tab") instead of a button, and some offer no next step at all.

In every option: one shared empty-state widget for the whole app. It always says what goes here, in one sentence, and why it's empty when that's not obvious. It has one button when there is a next step, such as "Add a phone line", "Connect Mailchimp" or "Open Settings". The button only shows to someone allowed to take that step; everyone else sees who can. Each module then replaces its own sentence with this widget, owner by owner.

1Centred, with an icon, a sentence and one button Recommended

In the middle of the area the list would fill: a soft round icon, a short bold line, one sentence, and the button. Why: the most common pattern in good apps, impossible to mistake for a broken screen, and the button sits where the eye lands.
