Human Resources · Dashboard

Jose, 2026-10-01: “the dashboard at human resources is also lacking, now we have a lot more information that could be displayed here”

Pick one desktop layout (1–3) and one phone layout (4–6), for example “1, 4”. A figure marked now ignores the period chips; one marked this year (or this week) follows them. Every number and every row opens the list it counts, in place.

What is wrong today

  • One card: a ring of active against inactive (one blue circle when almost nobody is inactive) and seven sentences. No sentence can be clicked, and none of them names anybody.
  • The server works out more than it sends. It counts onboardings still being filled in apart from those waiting on a signature, and stalled invitations that expired apart from those never opened; it has the new starters’ names and each certification’s person and date. The screen gets only the totals. A headcount by place is written in the code and never called.
  • “68 people joined this week” is wrong. Joined counts accounts created, not start dates, so the 64 people imported from A1 Tools on Sep 29 count as joining this week. Where A1 had a hire date, it is already on their record as the Start date.
  • Nothing from Work Hours or Training, although the module’s own line promises “who is on the clock”. Two thirds of the screen is empty.

1The figures, two charts and “right now”, then three lists Recommended

Six equal figures across the top, then who joined and left month by month, where people work, and who is on the clock right now. Underneath are three short lists with names: paperwork waiting on someone, the newest starters, and certifications that have lapsed or expire within 60 days. Click any figure or row and its whole list opens in a panel docked on the right.

Server: already worked out and dropped before it reaches the screen: the onboarding and invitation splits, the starters’ names and the certification rows. Headcount by place is written and never called. New: Joined counted by start date, joined and left per month, and the stalled invitations as a list. On the clock and Due for retaking are the existing Work Hours and Training boxes, shown only to people who may open those modules.

2What needs doing, first

A to-do list instead of a report. Each line names the person, says what is stuck and since when, and carries the button that fixes it: Sign, Resend, Open. The team’s figures sit in a narrow column on the right, and the last line says what was checked and found clear, with its window.

Server: new — whose turn each onboarding is (yours or the new hire’s), the stalled invitations as a list, one count of records missing details with the box most often empty, contracts signed in the period, and Joined by start date. Sign, Resend and the hours corrections already exist.

3Everyone, with where they are today

People first. One row per person: where they work, where they are today (on the clock, away, not in yet), hours this week, how long they have been here, and anything outstanding on their record. Every count above the list is a filter, and the person you click opens in a panel on the right with a link to their full record.

Server: new — one list that puts each person’s record gaps, paperwork, today’s clock and hours side by side (each lives on its own screen today), and work anniversaries from start dates. Not in yet compares the schedule with the clock, both of which Work Hours already has. Birthdays are left out on purpose: dates of birth are kept off every list today, so showing them is your call.
