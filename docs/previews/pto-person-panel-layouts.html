Jose (2026-10-22) asked for six changes:

  • Expired is always shown, even at 0, so the layout doesn't jump between people.
  • "When they can be used" replaces "When you can use them".
  • A more colourful reset section, as bars or a countdown.
  • Past resets are shown.
  • Changes appear as a timeline.
  • A role filter, plus which days were used, picked on a calendar where the days can be scattered.

Every name and date here is invented.

1 Four fixed tiles, a countdown ring with the period bar, a calendar for days off, and one timeline RECOMMENDED

  • The list: search, Everyone / Can use / Not yet, and a Role picker.
  • Tiles: Total, Used, Left and Expired are always there, so the layout never shifts. Zeros are grey. "Left" turns red at 0, and "Expired" is amber when it's above 0.
  • Reset card:
    • a countdown ring: "56 days" left until the next reset;
    • a bar for this period, from the last reset to the next, with today's position, the days used in blue and the days that will expire in amber if nothing is used;
    • past resets underneath, as chips: "Dec 17, 2025 · 2 expired".
  • "When they can be used" is reworded and sits beside the reset card.
  • Record days off replaces "Change". It opens an editing area under the tiles, with no side pane:
    • a month calendar where you tap any days, even scattered ones. Tapping a day again makes it a half day, and a third tap clears it;
    • days already recorded show light blue;
    • a running total: "3½ days · leaves 2½";
    • a reason, then Save.
    "Used" becomes the count of recorded days. "Correct the total instead" stays as a small link for a number that has no dates.
  • Timeline, newest first, one stream: days off (with their dates), corrections (who, from → to, reason), resets (the day and how many expired) and the start date.
