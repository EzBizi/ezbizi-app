Leads › Stats

A1 Leads takeover. The Leads server (2f83e1218) gives the numbers, and Field gives the map's offices and jobs. Every name and number here is invented.

What it shows for a period and a branch: leads, booked and the booking rate (a gauge); leads by source; the top markets; why leads were lost (status, and AI's reasons); services booked; a day × time-of-day heatmap; the dispatchers; and a map. The map has three layers you can switch on and off: offices (sized by leads, coloured by booking rate from red to green), leads by ZIP (filtered by outcome), and jobs.
