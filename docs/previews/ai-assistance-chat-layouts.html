AI Assistance › AI, as a chat

Jose, today: "the "AI" tab and that menu on the left aren't really meant to be together. The AI tab should just be the ask bar at the bottom, sort of like here where i can ask you stuff and the reply goes on top". So AI becomes a conversation. The ask bar is pinned at the bottom, and each question and its answer stack above it, newest last. The topics menu moves to Library. Every question and answer here is invented.

The server is unchanged: each question asks the Library and Grok, as the tab does now. An answer shows Grok's reply in the look Jose asked for on WhatsApp, with capitals at sentence starts and emojis in place of bullet dots. Under it go the Library entries it came from, as chips that open the entry, and any branch the question named, with Call and Map. The questions last only while the app is open, as now.
