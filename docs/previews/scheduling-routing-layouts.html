Scheduling › Routing, and Re-Scheduling's job types

Two of Jose's asks from today. A new Routing tab shows each day's recommended routes, one per technician. Re-Scheduling tags repairs and redos, and a row opens to show that day's jobs and where the moved one would fit. Every name, address and number here is invented.

The server is Scheduling's (GET /scheduling/routes?area_id&date). A technician takes at most 6 jobs a day. Each route has its stops in order with the time, the job and the customer, its miles (straight-line), and a Google Maps link that opens the real directions through those stops. Routes past the area's capacity are marked needs another technician. Jobs Workiz has no location for are listed under No location, so they aren't forgotten.
