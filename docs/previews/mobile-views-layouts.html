207 · Better phone views: Jobs, Task Board, Leads, Scheduling

Jose, 11-23, via the desk: "take inspiration for how stuff looks on mobile on workiz, monday, etc." The patterns worth taking are below. All names are invented.
Workiz: the day's schedule lists jobs, leads and tasks together, and each has quick actions for call, message, directions and reschedule. The job page has one-tap Clock in, Send ETA and Add payment.
monday.com: My Work sits in the bottom bar, a round + adds from anywhere, and the item card opens full screen with an Updates tab and a breadcrumb back to its board.
Every option keeps our bottom bar (four modules plus More) and the house pills and colours. Tap targets are 44 pt or more, and the main actions sit within thumb reach.

From Workiz. Each job card shows the number, status pill, customer, address and time, with Call, Text and Navigate one tap away. Inside a job, a sticky bar at the bottom holds Call, Text, Navigate and the next step (Start, then Done).
