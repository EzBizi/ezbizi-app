Jose, 11-23 (via the desk; backend from Customer Experience, ed182b56d). A technician on site types a ZIP, picks the client or types the job number, and taps Generate. A QR code comes up big enough for the customer to scan from the technician's phone. It opens that branch's smiley/sad review page, and every scan counts for the technician who made the code. The same screen is a sub-tab in Customer Experience (after Review links) and in Inspection (last). Review links gets a leaderboard showing which technician got the most reviews.

1 · One centred field that understands all three (recommended)

One field: "ZIP, client or job number". Five digits gives a "Use ZIP 44310" row. Anything else searches clients and jobs, and the matches drop down (a client with their address, a job with its number and client). Pick one, tap Generate, and the QR replaces the form, centred, with the branch's name under it. Then: Download PNG (for printing), Copy link, and New code to start over. One line says "Scans count for you. Valid until Nov 23, 2027."
