Project Management · totals strip

Today: three lines of plain text. Four ways to make the figures read at a glance. Paid = green, Remaining = amber (not red: an open balance is normal, not a problem), Total = brand. Figures from Jose's screenshot.

1 · Tinted stat tiles RECOMMENDED

Three equal tiles, the figure large. Reads fastest; equal-size siblings. On phone they stack as label-left, figure-right rows.

2 · Cards with icon and accent bar

White cards with a coloured left edge and icon. Quieter colour, more chrome; takes more height.

3 · Paid vs remaining bar

One card: the total, then a bar split paid/remaining with shares. Shows progress, which the tiles don't. Note: Paid + Remaining here ($987,778) is more than the total, so the bar uses each against their sum; worth checking the figures first.

4 · Compact pills

Smallest: one line of tinted pills. Fits above a list, but the figures are as small as today's.
