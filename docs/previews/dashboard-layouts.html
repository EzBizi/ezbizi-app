Dashboard · today first

Jose, 2026-10-01: “i also feel like the dashboard and it's widgets are a bit stale and may need some improvements, i also want the default to be Day ( as in today ) instead of Week”

Pick one desktop layout (1–3) and one phone layout (4–6), for example “1, 4”. In every one Day comes first and is chosen when you sign in. A figure marked today follows the chips; one marked now ignores them. Every number, row and card opens its module.

What is wrong today

  • Every module you can open is one equal card, at least 176 px tall, two to a row, each row as tall as its tallest card: up to 27 cards (22 live, 5 placeholders) in 14 rows, and one sentence takes the room of a chart.
  • The numbers sit inside sentences. The screen draws only rings, bars, lines and sentences; the big-number widget in the card file is used nowhere, nothing reads the server’s snapshot mark, and no card opens its module.
  • The rings say little. Jobs counts every job ever, finished ones included, so the chips never move it; one inspection name is one solid ring; Fleet, Phone, Email, Jobs and Automations send no sentence when nothing is due, so the screen writes “Nothing to show.” under a full chart (your Fleet box).
  • Five boxes say Coming soon (Newsletter, Monitoring, AI Assistance, Customer Experience, Profit & Loss) though all five modules are live.
  • “36,067 new this week” is mostly the A1 Tools import: Customers counts the day a row was written, and the importer keeps the real date in customer_since. HR’s Joined had the same fault and was fixed this morning (c2e55267). Jobs’ “coming up” includes the rest of today, and Scheduling still says “not what is booked” though bookings have been counted since Sep 29.

Every option keeps

  • One box per module you can open, gated as today by its permission and by the module being on for the business, in your saved order. Nobody can hide one; a module switched on later joins at the end.
  • Rearranging by drag, keyboard and touch, with today’s move buttons and grip, behind one Arrange button so a card is not four controls.
  • One period for the page, Day first and chosen by default. Figures that follow it say today (or this week, this month); snapshots (stock, vehicles, job statuses, who is on the clock) say now or carry no period word, so the chips never seem to move them.
  • Every sentence a box sends, in full, from none to about four. A box with only sentences, a statement or one that could not load, shows them muted with no chart: every option draws Marketing as the statement and Reports as the failure.
  • The chart kinds each module allows, under Arrange; Figures is a new kind and the default.
  • No money anywhere, tiles included (Jose, 2026-09-29): Done today is a count. No dialogs; a number opens its module on the list it counts.

Server, for all six: Day. DashboardPeriod gains day (midnight to now, in the view’s time zone) and a phrase that writes “today”; a peer has exactly this uncommitted in the tree as of this drawing, with the default moved from month to day. Two catches. A stored choice beats the default, and yours is stored as Week, so stored periods need resetting once. And bucket() puts a whole day in one bucket, so on Day every line chart there is today (Customers, Leads, Reviews, Marketing, A1Leads GBP) is a single dot; these designs show today beside the 14 days before it instead.

Server, for all six: sentences. These designs turn a box’s sentences into figures and to-do rows, which breaks the every-sentence rule as written: each box has to send figures and attention rows instead of, or beside, its sentences. A sentence that is neither (HR’s note on leaving dates, a placeholder’s) is still drawn whole. A box that could not load arrives exactly like a statement (empty and one sentence), so it can only be drawn like one unless the server marks it.

1Today’s six numbers, what needs you, then each module in figures Recommended

Six numbers for today across the top. A card for each module that changes during the day, with two or three figures and the last 14 days (or the next 7) behind them. On the right, everything that needs somebody, most urgent first, and what was checked and found clear. Underneath, compact boxes for the modules that change slowly and for any box that has only sentences right now: a statement such as Marketing’s, a placeholder, or one that could not load, like Reports here. Each shows every sentence it was sent, and a row of them is as tall as its tallest. Why this one: it answers the morning’s three questions in the order they are asked (how is today going, what needs me, how is each part doing), the same shape as the HR dashboard you chose, and every piece of it comes from one change to the boxes.

Server. Sent today, but only inside sentences, rings or bars: jobs today and coming up; leads arrived and not dealt with; calls answered and missed, voicemails waiting; on the clock and hours; customers listed; reviews arrived, unanswered and their average; inspections submitted and in progress; weekly capacity; unread per inbox; and the compact boxes’ counts. New: figures and attention rows from every box, and a flag for the compact ones. Done today (jobs whose earned_at falls today: a count, never an amount, and zero until a business fills in jobs.earned_statuses, which starts empty). After today apart from today. Waiting for a reply and booked, which GET /leads/dashboard already works out and the Leads box does not. Overbooked days and full areas from Overbooking, which the Scheduling box does not read. Jobs booked per day for the next 7, the 14 days behind each today figure, and new customers by customer_since. Jobs in the period too (the Week window draws 229 this week), counted over the whole day or week because booked work lies ahead, where every other period figure runs to now; today the Jobs box ignores the chips, and its status counts stay a snapshot. Saved chart choices beat a new default, as the period does.

Drawn as an owner might set it, with Jobs, Leads, Phone and Work Hours large. Boxes of one size share a row, so a row is always equal cards, and a compact box shows every sentence it was sent: Marketing is a statement and Reports could not load.

Server: 1’s figures and attention rows (here drawn inside each box), plus three things. Day by the hour for the large boxes: jobs by start time split done, in progress and to go, leads by arrival, calls answered and missed, which is an hourly arm in bucket() for day. Who is on the clock per place, for Work Hours (the box sends two counts; the Work Hours screen has the rest). And a size per box in the saved layout: storedEntry keeps only the key and the chart kind today, so the save has to accept and keep a size.
