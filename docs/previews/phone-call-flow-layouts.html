Read from call_flow_pane.dart “What actually happens when somebody rings this business.” Read-only — everything here is set on other screens, which is why it has no save footer under the approved pane pattern. One card per line, each showing that line's resolved steps, plus badges for recording and transcription. It already carries an alarm when lines reach nobody, in the errorContainer-plus-a-count vocabulary Phone set: “One line reaches nobody. Every call to it goes straight to voicemail.” Empty state exists and is kept.
Two things about this pane that decided the recommendation 1 · It is the report of the chain General configures, and the code says so — General leads, Call flow follows, “the form you fill in, then the report of what that did”. So the resolved path here should look like the chain there. Same four steps, same numbers, same treatment for a step that gets skipped. Layouts 1 and 3 do that literally; layout 2 does it in miniature, left to right. 2 · The alarm counts the broken lines and then makes you hunt for them. “One line reaches nobody” is the right sentence, but with eleven lines the next question is which one, and today the answer is scroll and look. Every layout here puts broken lines first and marks them in place — the count and the location should not be two separate discoveries. The thing that decides between 1 and 2 is how many lines a business has. A-1 has eleven. Eleven vertical ladders is a very long scroll for a screen you open to check one thing; eleven table rows is a glance. If most customers run two or three lines, layout 1 is the better answer and I would switch my recommendation — that is a fact about your customers, not about the design, and you know it and I do not.
