AI in EzBizi · one marker, and where each AI feature lives

From the Grok AI session, 2026-10-12. Jose approved an AI program today, and the AI session asked UI/UX for two things. First, where each of eight AI features lives. Second, a marker that tells a person what AI decided or wrote. Five of the features are new. Three exist today with no marker: the name and ZIP AI fills in on a lead, its lead sort and flags, and the call labels in Performance. A fourth is half there: "Draft with AI" in the review reply editor.

Same in every option. The glyph is auto_awesome ✦, which EzBizi already uses on Draft with AI. The marker always says AI in words, never by colour alone. Hovering or a long press shows where it came from and how to change it ("Set by AI from the call at 2:14 PM · edit it to change it"). When a person edits an AI value, the marker goes away, because the value is now theirs; the history still records who wrote what. AI never sends anything to a customer by itself: a reply stays a draft until a person presses Send. AI's guesses are worded as guesses ("Sounds like a complaint", not "Complaint"), and each has a one-tap "not right" that corrects it. Names, calls and numbers here are invented.

The marker, three ways

1 · A chip and a card badge RECOMMENDED

One marker in two sizes. After a value AI set, a small AI chip. At the top of something AI wrote, a badge that names it.

2 · A tint

Everything AI wrote sits on a pale band with a teal rule down its side. Values AI set have a dotted underline. It reads quickly once learned, but a band says nothing until someone has learned it.

3 · The glyph only

Just the ✦ after an AI value or heading, with one legend line per screen ("✦ set by AI"). The quietest of the three, but the easiest to miss on a busy screen.

1AI where the work already is, marked with the chip RECOMMENDED

Nothing moves to a new place. A call's summary and next step become the top of its card in the lead's conversation, with Show full transcript under them, which is Jose's order. The name and ZIP AI filled carry the chip. A suspected complaint is a red strip across the top of the lead with Not a complaint and Take it; the lead manager and administrators get a notification. A reply draft lands in the composer with its badge and is only ever sent by a person.

2AI in one place: an "AI" tab on the lead, an "AI" page in Reports

Everything AI made sits behind a tab: the lead gets Conversation · AI, and Reports gets an AI page for scoring and coaching. The marker is the tint. Nothing AI wrote mixes with what people wrote.

Cost: Jose asked for the summary first, above the transcript. Here it is a tab away, so it is read only by people who go looking. A complaint in a tab nobody opens is a complaint missed. A new tab also needs its own grant (the registry guard), which is the Social split again.

Recommendation: 1 and 4. AI's work goes where the work already is, and the marker is one component in two sizes, so an administrator learns it once. The chip says AI in words, the tooltip says how to change it, and the strip and drafts are worded so nothing AI guessed reads as fact. Build: AiMark (the chip and the badge, with the tooltip) goes in client/lib/widgets/, which is mine. Each feature's owner places it when the feature lands, and for the three that exist today I mark them myself once their owners agree.
