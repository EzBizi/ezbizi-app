Leads · managing connections

Jose, 2026-09-30, on Leads › Manage: “i feel we could also use a revamp on the manage of leads, see what you can do about it”.

Today Manage opens one narrow panel with every provider stacked: Google Ads (22 accounts), then Thumbtack, then Website. Each account row carries three text buttons (Name it, Change, Disconnect) that wrap onto a second line. Certification problems are loose red lines under the row. Disconnect all sits next to the search box. Nothing says how many accounts need you until you scroll through all 22.

Every option keeps: the name you give an account with its number beside it, the Google id that tells twins apart, where each account's leads go (and “Placed somewhere you cannot see”), the lead count, the certification check, the fold for accounts that have never sent a lead, and disconnecting confirmed in place. A person who can only view Leads sees the same page with no buttons. No new server fields: “Needs attention” is a certification in attention or a broken or revoked connection, and “Never sent a lead” is a lead count of 0, both served today.

1A Connections page in place: providers, a table, the account beside it Recommended

Manage opens a page inside Leads, with ← back to the leads. Providers on the left, each with its count and how many need attention. The selected provider's accounts fill a table (Account, Goes to, Leads, Status) with chips for All · Needs attention · Never sent a lead. Clicking a row opens the account beside the table: its name, where its leads go (a search picker), its certification with each problem on its own line, and Disconnect at the foot. Rows carry no buttons. Disconnect all moves to the foot of the table. This is the list-and-panel shape Estimates and Reports already use.
