Clock In · the lock screen

Jose, 2026-09-30: “the clock in lock screen could also use some love, show the app logo, etc.”

Today: the word “EzBizi” in plain type, the time, the name, a raw “08:00 – 17:00”, a sentence, a green button and a Sign out link, small in the middle of an empty dark window. Every option keeps the behaviour: it takes the window until the punch succeeds, the after-hours sentence (“one extension, and the sweep closes it”), the error under the button, and Sign out. Every option adds the app mark (the icon and the Ez/Bizi wordmark the sign-in page uses), today’s date, the person’s photo or initials and role, and the shift written in the organization’s own clock (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, not 08:00 – 17:00). Client only: all of this is already in /me.
