Customer Experience asked (Shoval via Jose, 11-20; server 22e04f82d). Complaints becomes Reviews & Complaints, the board Adeline and the GMs work from. What's new: BBB, Attorney General and lawsuit complaints are priority, due 24 h after they come in. Each complaint gets a Client / Not a client mark. "New complaint" is for things entered by hand. A Google review from someone who isn't a client gets a one-click Report on Google: Google's API can't report a review, so the button opens Google's Reviews Management Tool and copies the evidence. The list, board, filters and side pane stay as they are.

1 · A "Due within 24 h" strip on top, badges on every row (recommended)

Open priority complaints sit in a red-edged strip above the list, soonest first. Each shows time left ("5 h left"), and the time turns solid red once it's overdue. They also stay in the list. Kind chips sit under the filters: All · Google · Website · Call · BBB · AG · Lawsuit · Other. Rows get a Client / Not a client badge and, on priority ones, the same clock.
"New complaint" opens a side panel with kind, title, summary, branch, when it came in and Client yes/no. Picking BBB, AG or Lawsuit says it'll be due in 24 h. In the pane, a not-a-client Google review shows the evidence and a Report on Google button (it opens Google and copies the evidence, then says "Copied, paste it in Google's form").
