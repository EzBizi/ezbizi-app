⚠ His list contains two different screens, and that is the thing to decide. The first bullet asks for one expanding row per user — a single list you open in place. The last asks for a left sidebar of users with the week beside it. Both are reasonable, they solve different problems, and building one does not give you the other. Options 1–3 are the three honest readings.

Facts the drawing has to respect, from Clock In’s brief rather than guessed:

• Everybody in reach is in the list, including people who worked nothing. A name missing from a sidebar reads as “not on the team”, which is not what “did not clock in” means — so zero-hour people are drawn, greyed, never dropped.
• An open shift reports time worked SO FAR, not null, because it is summed into the total beside it. Green means still running.
• Times arrive already formatted in the organization’s clock and 12/24h setting. Nothing here formats from the device.
• Sunday is a default, not a rule. The week start is an organization setting, so the header reads starts_on rather than assuming Sunday.

Week navigation is not on his list, and every option draws it anyway. The tab this replaces has day arrows, so shipping without it would take away something he has today. Say if you would rather it went.
