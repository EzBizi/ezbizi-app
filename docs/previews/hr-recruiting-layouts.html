201 · HR › Recruiting

HR asked (Shoval, owner, 11-20; server 9dfa1e434). This is a new HR tab, Recruiting. Applicants come from every source and move through stages. Each has a "Relevant" flag and phone or in-person interviews with an outcome, and the monthly spend is per position. It's gated on hr.view, and hr.edit is needed to change anything, so the write controls are hidden without it. Positions, stages and sources are the organization's lists (Settings › Lists), and their labels come from the server. No dialogs.

1 · A list with stage chips, the applicant docked beside it; Spend as a sub-tab (recommended)

This is the house list (Leads, Daily approval). The sub-tabs are Applicants · Spend. Above the list are the search bar and pickers for position, stage, source, Company · Branch and dates, plus "+ Applicant". Chips show applicants, relevant, interviewed, hired and the median first response. Rows show name and contact, position, source, a stage pill, the relevant mark, interviews, applied and first response.
Clicking a row docks the applicant beside the list. The panel has details you edit in place, a Relevant switch, a stage picker and the interviews (each with kind, time, held, outcome and interviewer), plus "+ Interview". Spend is a month × position grid you edit in the cell, with each position's total, cost per hire (spend ÷ hires) and "+ Spend".
