Reports › Performance › People: answer and booking, apart

Jose (11-03): "a column for not answered and answered, missed call, separate from the booked, not booked". What's wrong today: one Result pill mixes Answered, Booked, Not booked and Missed call, and the People table has four answer columns (Rang, Answered, Teammate took, Nobody answered) and no booking figures. Every name here is invented.

1 Two columns: Answer, then Booking RECOMMENDED

  • People table: Rang · Answered · Not answered · Missed · Booked · Booking rate. "Not answered" means a teammate took the call; "Missed" means nobody did. Booking rate is booked out of answered.
  • A person's calls: an Answer pill (Answered / Not answered · who took it / Missed) and a Booking pill (Booked / Not booked / Existing job). Booking is "—" when the call wasn't answered, so it never counts against them.
  • Filters: two chip groups, Answer and Booking, so "Answered, Not booked" is two taps. Red is used only for Missed.
