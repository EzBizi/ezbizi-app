Financials › Invoices: charge sheets, invoices and settings

Jose said yes to invoicing on 2026-10-21. Invoices is a new tab in Financials, and inside it there are three sub-tabs:

  • Charge sheets: what each branch owes for a period.
  • Invoices: the invoices made from those sheets.
  • Settings: charge lines, how emails go out, due days and numbering.

Every name and number here is invented.

1 Sheet as a spreadsheet; invoices as a list with a preview pane RECOMMENDED

  • Charge sheets:
    • a period picker (one sheet a month) beside "New sheet";
    • the grid has a row per branch (Total jobs, then a column per charge line) and totals on both edges;
    • cells are typed in like Financials tables (Tab, Enter, paste);
    • "Make invoices · 3 branches" drafts one invoice per branch with a total above zero. A branch already invoiced for that period is skipped and says so.
  • Invoices:
    • totals on top: Sent this period, Unpaid, Overdue;
    • filters for status, branch and period;
    • each row shows number, branch, period, total, due date, amount due and a status chip (Draft, Approved, Sent, Paid, Overdue, Void).
  • The invoice pane (a side pane):
    • steps across the top: Draft → Approved → Sent → Paid;
    • the preview, laid out like the PDF;
    • lines editable only while it's a draft;
    • the next action as the main button, with Void under "More". Void needs a reason. A sent invoice can never be deleted.

  • Send… changes the pane to show the recipients (the branch's billing contacts, ticked, plus "Add an address"), the subject, and a preview of the email with the summary table and the PDF attached. Then Send.
  • Record payment asks for date, amount, method and reference. A negative amount is a refund. Payments are listed in the pane with who recorded each one.
  • Without the approve permission, the Approve button doesn't appear for you, and a sentence says who can approve.

2 Invoices open as a full page, like a document

Clicking an invoice replaces the list with the invoice at page size, with the actions in a bar on top. It's the most like a printed invoice, but you lose the list, and checking five invoices takes five back-and-forths.

3 One screen: the sheet with each branch's invoice beside it

Charge sheets and Invoices merge: each sheet row ends with its invoice's status chip and Approve, Send or Paid buttons. It's compact for one month, but finding last quarter's unpaid invoices means opening each sheet.
