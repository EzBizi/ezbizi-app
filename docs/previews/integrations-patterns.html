Integrations · patterns from other apps

Four layouts, each copying a pattern that a well-known app actually ships (sources linked). Same data in all four: checks with their reason and last-checked time, branch, choices made at connect, and Fix / Re-run. Red only for broken.

A · Attention block plus per-app rollup RECOMMENDED

Borrows HubSpot's "Needs your attention" block and Zapier's Apps tab ("N connections · M expired", expands into the account list, "used by" counts). HubSpot · Zapier

B · App cards plus a Manage panel

Borrows GoHighLevel's Integrations revamp: cards that roll up status, Manage lists every account, and a Default account per branch. GoHighLevel

C · Account page: checks with reason, fix and next retry

Borrows Stripe's webhook deliveries (error → fix table, next retry) and Segment's expandable error rows (sample request/response). This is the detail page that A or B opens. Stripe · Segment

D · Problems queue with bulk Re-run

Borrows Jobber's sync dashboard (errors vs warnings, "Resolve (#)" on the filtered set, "Waiting to sync") and Housecall Pro's header alert. Jobber · Housecall Pro

Not copied: ServiceTitan and Workiz (no documented health screen; Workiz has you paste tokens by hand). Shopify, Slack and Notion show no health at all.
