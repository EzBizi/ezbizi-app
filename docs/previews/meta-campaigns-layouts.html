Meta campaigns control

Jose, 11-04: "we need to be able to control meta campaigns from the app." This goes on the existing Ads performance screen, behind a Google | Meta switch. The data comes from GET meta-ads/tree (campaign › ad set › ad), with POST status and POST budget. ads_performance.view lets you read; ads_performance.manage shows the switches and the budget edit. Every name is invented (Brightside, per city).

1 One expandable table, switch on each row RECOMMENDED

  • The Google | Meta segmented control sits beside the range picker. Meta has its own account picker when more than one account is connected.
  • Campaign rows expand to ad sets, and ad sets expand to ads. Each row has an on/off switch. A row switched on but held by its parent shows a dimmed switch and "Paused by campaign" (from effective_status).
  • The budget is a link only on the row that owns it (daily_budget not null). The other rows say "Campaign budget" or "Ad set budgets" in grey. Tapping the link opens a SidePanel.
  • Pausing takes effect at once with an Undo snackbar. No dialog.
