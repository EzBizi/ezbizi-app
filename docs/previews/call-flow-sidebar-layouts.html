Editing from the call flow — pick one desktop and one mobile

Jose, 2026-09-16: “if i were to click on the ring group at the top as u can see in the image, it should open a right sidebar with the list of members, so i can assign - unassign, so basically, every settings needs to be synced and manageable from here as well”.

This is the ask I parked yesterday, and he has now made it twice. It reverses the read-only scope he set with “shows me”, so it is settled: the chart becomes somewhere you change things. What is NOT settled is what the drawer is, and the three answers cost very different amounts and age very differently.

The hinge is the second half of his sentence. “A list of members I can assign and unassign” is one editor. “Every settings manageable from here” is fourteen — Phone stamps every node with edit: {pane, id}, where pane is a real section id (ring-groups, voicemail, hours, flows, messaging…). Whether the drawer hosts those panes or reimplements them is the whole decision.

⚠ One thing worth knowing before picking 2. A bespoke editor per node kind is the nicest to use and the one that goes wrong quietly: the day somebody adds a field to ring groups in Settings, this drawer still shows yesterday’s fields and nothing fails. Option 1 cannot drift, because there is only one of it.

Phone has offered to put anything else on a node — permissions, a short label, whether a node is editable at all — rather than have me derive it. So if a layout here needs a fact it does not have, that is a question rather than a blocker.
