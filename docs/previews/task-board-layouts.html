Task Board · boards for every company, franchise and person

From the Task Board session, relaying Jose: a "Task Board" tab, Monday.com-style. Tied to Organization → Company → Franchise. Every Company, every Franchise, and every user automatically gets its own board, assigned to them. Must work with Automations (triggers/actions) and Reports. They will build the backend and wire Automations and Reports to whichever layout Jose picks.

Same in every option.

  • Task Board is its own module in the sidebar, with an icon, the way Jobs is.
  • The top-bar view decides which boards show. With a franchise chosen, you see your own board, that franchise's board, and its company's. With a company chosen, you see the company's board and the franchise boards under it. A board outside the view never shows.
  • A board is groups (coloured, foldable sections such as This week, Next week, Done) of tasks. Each task has an owner, a status coloured the way Monday does it (Not started / Working on it / Stuck / Done, always with the word as well as the colour), a due date (late in red), and a link to an EzBizi record (job, lead or customer), so a task can say what it is about.
  • A task opens in the side panel: its fields, its updates (comments) and its history. You add a task by typing on the "+ Add task" row at the foot of a group.
  • Automations can be triggered when a task is created, changes status, or passes its due date, and can create a task, change its status or assign it. Reports get tasks by status, owner and board, plus overdue. The Task Board session builds those; the screens below are where their results appear.
Names, tasks and dates are invented.

1Boards as tabs across the top, table or kanban RECOMMENDED

The boards in this view are a row of tabs: My board first (where Task Board opens), then the franchise, then the company. Each tab shows how many open tasks are yours. Under them, Table (Monday's main view) or Kanban by status. Switching the top-bar view swaps which franchise and company tabs show, and My board stays.
