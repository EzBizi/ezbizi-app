Already decided, not in this gallery Availability means dispatch capacity — how many jobs a franchise can take, per slot, per weekday, per service area. A franchise creates service areas holding zip codes; one zip belongs to exactly one area, enforced. Slots and capacity are defined per area, not franchise-wide. A closed day is a day with no slot rows — closing deletes, it does not zero. Capacity is per weekday and recurring: there are no per-date overrides and none are being designed.
