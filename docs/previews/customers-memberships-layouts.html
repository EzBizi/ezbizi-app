Customers · Memberships · Dashboard, Members and Settings

Jose, 2026-10-05: a yearly maintenance program, "a sub tab in Customers, with its own sub tabs of Dashboard - Members - Settings", and on Members "customer list, enrollment type, plan type, amount due, state, zip, estimate gross for client, amount paid so far". His word for the pick, the same day: "go with ur recommended options as usual".

Data: all of it is served on the Membership branch (19240797): GET /memberships rows (customer, enrollment, plan, billing, due, state, zip, estimated gross, paid, status, starts and ends), POST/PATCH/DELETE, and the plans. Amounts come back empty without jobs.financials, and then their columns and tiles are not drawn at all, rather than drawn as dashes. Dashboard figures are counted from the same rows.

1Tiles over the members table, a member opened in place Recommended

Members is Jose's columns, in his order, under four tiles that are also the filters: active, renewing in the next 30 days, lapsed, and amount due (money only for financials). A row opens the member in place with a ← back: this year, what has been paid (changed right there), every earlier year, and Cancel. Add a member opens a side panel: customer, plan, start date. Dashboard is the same figures with what is coming up: renewals in the next 30 days by name, and members by plan. Settings is the plans, each changed in its row, plus where the renewal reminder is set. Why: it is the shape of every other list in the app (Inventory, Connection), the columns are the ones Jose named, and nothing opens over the module.
