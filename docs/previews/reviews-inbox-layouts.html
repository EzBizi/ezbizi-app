1 Inbox: the list on the left, the review and its reply on the right RECOMMENDED

  • Summary strip: average rating (this month vs last), new this month, unanswered, and 1–2 star reviews needing a reply. Each one filters the list.
  • Filters: search plus quick chips (Unanswered · 1–2 stars · This week). Rating, time, location, match and status sit behind one "More filters".
  • Each review row shows:
    • a letter avatar, the name, stars (red when 1–2), the location as a small tag and the time;
    • one line of the review;
    • a red dot when it isn't answered yet.
    The customer match only shows when it's useful: "Matched to Dana Reyes" or "No customer match · Link".
  • The right pane:
    • the full review;
    • who it is (the match, the job and the technician named in it, e.g. "Max");
    • a reply box with "Draft with AI", plus Post reply;
    • a notes field;
    • status: Open, Handled or Escalated to complaint.
  • Board view stays as a toggle.
