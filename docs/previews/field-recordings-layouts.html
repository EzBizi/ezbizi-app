Field: safety recordings

Field and the deploy desk, 2026-10-16: safety audio moves over from A1 Tools. Two parts: a Record control for technicians on their phones, and a Recordings tab. Every name here is invented.

Rules the screens follow: recording stops by itself when the phone leaves the job, or after 4 hours. In a state where the customer must be told (an organization setting; FL, CA, PA, IL and CT by default), the line to say comes first. Stop asks why: Done, The customer asked or Personal. A recording is deleted after 90 days unless somebody keeps it with a reason.

1–3 The Record control, on a technician's phone

1 (recommended): a card in Field › Dashboard, right under the SOS banner, showing the job it's for. While recording, it shows the time and the Pause and Stop buttons, and when the signal drops it reads "2 of 5 minutes sent". 2: a red round button that floats over every Field screen, with the same controls in a sheet that slides up. Always within reach, but it covers part of the list. 3: a bar pinned to the top of the app while recording, like a phone call. Hard to miss, but it's only there once recording has started, so Start still needs one of the other two.

4 Recordings tab: a table, and play in a side panel RECOMMENDED

Filters for person and day. Each row shows the person, the job, when it started, how long it ran, any gaps (for example a phone call) and when it is deleted, or "Kept" with the reason. Clicking a row opens a side panel with a player that runs the one-minute pieces as one recording, a timeline marking the gaps, and Keep or Stop keeping (Field managers, with a reason). Technicians see only their own.

6 Recordings on the job itself

No new tab. Each job's page gets a Recordings section, and Field › Recordings is only a short list of the kept ones. Close to the work, but there's no single place to review a day.

On a phone

The Recordings tab with option 4 becomes a list (person, job, length, kept). Tapping a recording opens it in place with a back arrow, containing the player, the gaps and Keep.
