Jose 2026-10-09, with a screenshot of a lead's thread: "i wonder if we can make this look a bit better". Today each call is a card with "Call · 3:14", a small letter-spaced line ("Customer called · 11:18 AM · 19m ago"), a play bar that reads "--:--" until it loads, and the words in a grey box headed "Transcript". The cards are different widths: one without words shrinks to the bar. Every name and line below is invented.

In every option: every call is the same width as the thread. It says who called and how it went with an icon and colour: the customer called (green) or we called (blue), and who. The time is easy to read, on the right ("11:18 AM · 19m ago"). The player shows the call's real length before it loads ("0:00 / 3:14", never "--:--"). The words read as a quote with a thin rule, not a grey box, and show 3 lines, then Read all. "Transcribing…" becomes a small chip with a spinner. Nothing changes on the server; the length is already on the call.
