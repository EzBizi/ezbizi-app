Leads › Manage › Office hours

From Jose via the desk (2026-10-22). Nobody had set office hours, so every place ran Mon–Fri 8–5 Eastern, and customers got "we're closed" at 6 PM. Each company and branch now sets its own days, times and time zone, and inherits from the place above it until it's changed. Every name here is invented.

1 A table of every place; Edit opens the week right under its row RECOMMENDED

  • Rows: the organization, then each company, with its branches indented under it.
  • Columns:
    • Hours as a short summary ("Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00 · Sat 9–13"). An inherited value is written in grey italics with "from A-1 Chimney".
    • Time zone, inherited or not, shown the same way.
    • "Own" chip where the place has set its own.
  • Warning: a place still on the default hours (nobody set them) gets a yellow notice at the top: "6 places use the default Mon–Fri 8–5 Eastern."
  • Edit opens the row in place, with no side pane. The week appears as one line of seven small day boxes, so you can see the whole week at a glance:
    • each day box has its open and close times, or Closed (click the day name to switch it);
    • "Copy Monday to weekdays";
    • a time zone picker;
    • "Use A-1 Chimney's hours again" to go back to inheriting.
  • "Used by" under the table lists the auto-replies that read these hours, each linking to its rule.
