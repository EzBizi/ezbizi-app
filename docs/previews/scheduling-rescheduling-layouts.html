Scheduling › Re-Scheduling, and the Availability map

Two of Jose's asks from today. The new Re-Scheduling tab lists booked jobs that would sit better on another day, beside other work. The Availability map gets a card for each dot and zoom buttons. Every name, address and number here is invented.

The server is Scheduling's (GET /scheduling/reschedules). Each row is one booked job, with the day and window it would fit better, how near the closest other job is now (or that it is alone that day), how near it would be, and the jobs it would sit beside. Rows come biggest saving first. Moving a job changes the customer's appointment, so in every option the suggestion is shown first and Move is a separate, confirmed step. It uses the existing reschedule call and only appears for people who can edit the schedule.

1 A list of moves beside a map RECOMMENDED

One row per suggestion: the job, from → to, how near it is now and how near it would be, and the miles saved. Pick a row and the right side shows that job on the suggested day's map with its new neighbours, the before and after, and Move.

7 Availability map: a card on each dot RECOMMENDED

Hover a dot (or tap it on a phone) and a card shows the job: customer, service, Workiz number, the time and technicians, its status ("Online booking" for a web hold), the address and how far it is. A click keeps the card open until you click elsewhere. + − ⤢ sit in the map's top-right corner (⤢ fits every dot again). The same card and buttons go on Re-Scheduling's map.
