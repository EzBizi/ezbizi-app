Marketing · Social posting that feels finished

From Jose (verbatim, 2026-09-28, via the Meta session): "there was no hint that the post had been successful … we need to add flare, loading screens, success screens … i didn't see anything appear on the calendar even though you posted it and it succeeded … the user needs to feel like he's accomplished what he set out to do like posting, scheduling".

In every option:
  • A visible "posting" moment: one line per account that resolves on its own: posted (with the network's View on Facebook link), failed (with the network's own sentence and Try again), or sent to drafts (TikTok: "finish it in the TikTok app").
  • A success moment that names what happened: "Posted to 3 accounts" or "Scheduled for Tuesday 9:00 AM on 3 accounts", with the post itself shown back and a way to see it on the calendar.
  • The calendar shows every post, sent and scheduled, on its day: green is posted, blue is scheduled, red is a failure to look at. The new one is outlined the first time you see it. (Today the calendars are drawn with no posts passed in; the data is already in GET /social/posts.)
  • No dialogs and no popup menus: the moment happens in the composer, in a page, or in a panel.

1The composer becomes the result, and the post lands on the calendar recommended

The three columns stay (pages · compose · calendars). Pressing Publish now turns the composer into a live list of the accounts, each resolving in turn; when the last one answers, a green check and "Posted to 3 accounts" with the post shown back, links per network, and Write another. The post appears on today's cell at the same moment, outlined once.
