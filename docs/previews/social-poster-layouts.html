Social poster: views, live progress and a post's detail

Jose, 2026-10-15: "starting with the Social Poster that rodrigo build to enhance our current one", "on the posted screen i want to have the links so i can verify that they were posted" and "ability to delete them". Every name here is invented.

What we have today: a list with Scheduled / Sent / Failed tiles. Each account's line already has "View on Facebook" when it posted, and "Try again" when it failed. What is missing: an "All" tile; other ways to see the posts; live progress while a post goes out; a way to edit, reschedule or cancel a scheduled post; a way to dismiss one failed account or delete a post from the network; and an honest "partly posted" when some accounts refused it.

1 The list stays, with a view switch and a detail panel RECOMMENDED

Tiles: All · Scheduled · Publishing · Posted · Failed. A switch for List | Board | Calendar, remembered per person. While anything is going out, the Social tab shows a pulsing dot and the card shows "Posting 2 of 3" with a bar. A post that went to some accounts and not others says Partly posted. Clicking a post opens a side panel with every account's result: a link to the live post, the network's own reason when it failed, and Try again or Dismiss. A scheduled post gets Edit, Reschedule and Cancel. Delete from the network appears only on accounts whose network allows it; on the others it says to delete it in that app.

With any option

Delete from the network asks first and says it can't be undone. Cancel on a scheduled post means it never goes out. Dismiss hides one failed account's line without retrying. Which networks allow deleting comes from the Marketing session; for Instagram and TikTok, the drawing assumes they don't.
