Phone · the Dial screen

Jose, 2026-09-30, on Communication › Phone › Dial: “see what we can do to improve the Phone Main Screen”.

What is wrong today, from his two screenshots and the code:

  • Recent calls are numbers, not people. The same number is listed nine times in a row. A number that belongs to a customer still shows as digits, although the server already matches it (customer_id on every call). The list is not grouped by day, and there is no way to show only missed calls.
  • The keypad floats in empty space. On a wide window the keypad is a small island. The grey box under it is where the live transcript goes, and it sits empty until a call starts.
  • The number is shown raw (+13055550107) in the box and again in the Customer panel. The panel says “Customer” and then shows only that number, with “No messages” at the top and the message box at the very bottom.
  • “Calling as” runs the full width of the column, and “Do not disturb” sits alone on a line of its own.

Every option keeps: the searchable Calling as picker, Do not disturb reported here and switched from the top bar, Page and Availability, the “what was it about” tag after a call, draggable columns, and pinning the person panel. Server work, every option: customer_name beside the customer_id each call already carries. Grouping by day, collapsing repeats and “called back” are worked out from the rows already sent.
