⚠ TWO DEFECTS VISIBLE IN HIS SCREENSHOT THAT HE DID NOT MENTION. Both get fixed whichever option wins.

1 — Two edge labels are painted on top of each other. At the queue junction his screen reads Ееrybody bese: that is Everyone busy and Waited too long drawn at the same point. FlowWirePainter puts each label at the midpoint of its own wire and has no idea another label is already there, so wherever wires converge — which is every line with a queue, 11 of 13 by Phone's count — they collide. Not a glitch: a missing rule.

And the rule has two halves — I got it wrong once here, and measured the page rather than trusting how it looked. My first pass put every label near its wire's start, reasoning that a fan separates early. It does not: a fan-out has barely left the source there. Measured on this page, that gave the five menu keys a pitch of 13px for text 11px tall — one pixel of air, which reads as a stack. Labelling a fan-out near its destination instead gives 67px. So: a wire leaving a node with several outgoing wires is labelled near its far end; a wire arriving at a node with several incoming wires — Jose's queue, where his screenshot collides — is labelled near its near end; anything else in the middle. Plus a plate behind every label so no wire runs through the text.

The wider columns are a second fix for a different problem, and I nearly credited them with the first. Changing both at once hid which one worked: measured, the column width moves the label pitch by nothing at all — 13px narrow and 13px wide under the old rule, 67px under the new one either way. What the width is for is horizontal room: the widest label on this line is Waited too long at 72px, and the old 58px gap between two 150px nodes left it overhanging by 14px onto the boxes. At 112px it clears by 40. The chart is now wider than the window and scrolls sideways, which his current screen already does.

2 — The menu node is five times taller than every other node, because it renders the entire prompt: “To schedule an appointment press 1, current customer please press 2, if you know your parties extension…”. That single box is what pushes the whole fan down and right, and it is a large part of why the chart does not fit on screen. Every option caps the detail at two lines and reveals the rest on the node itself. The prompt is worth reading once, not every time the tab opens.
