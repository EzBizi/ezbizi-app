208 · The phone's bottom bar, chosen per role

Jose, 11-23, via the desk. Each role picks up to 4 slots for the phone's bottom bar, and More stays fixed as the fifth. A slot can be a whole module (Jobs) or one of its tabs (Field › Map, Jobs › Estimates), so a technician's bar can be their quick tools. It's set in Roles & Permissions with a live phone mock that changes as you pick. The picker only offers what that role can reach. An empty slot falls back to today's default (Jobs, Scheduling, Leads, Phone). Example role: Technician.

1 · Four slot pickers beside a live phone (recommended)

A "Mobile bar" card in the role editor, under the permissions. The phone on the right redraws on every change, with slot 1 shown as open. "Reset to default" clears it.

How the bar renders: phone (4), iPhone Duo folded and open

A tab slot uses its module's icon and the tab's short label, and opens the module on that tab. The bar is the same on the folded outer screen and the open inner screen (design 206). The sidebar takes over only from 900 wide (the Duo sideways, iPad, desktop). On a phone, the role-editor card stacks the mock above the four pickers.
