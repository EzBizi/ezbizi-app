199 · Reports › nine operations reports

Reports asked (Shoval, owner, 11-18, via the desk). Nine new reports: Tech AI checks, Tech report, Cancelled jobs, Complaints KPI, Dispatch, Accounting weekly, Recruiting, Pending jobs and Estimates. All of them share the shape of today's Marketing, Meta, Thumbtack and Jobs tabs: the date bar, figures, search with Company · Branch, technician and source filters, then a grid with Download and pinned headings. A row opens the job or person, and each report has its own role grant. Reports already has 9 tabs, and nine more would make 18. The options below are mostly about grouping. Cancelled jobs is drawn as the sample because its data is ready.

1 · Three family tabs with sub-tabs (recommended)

Each report joins the family it belongs to, as Project Management Settings does with its sub-tabs. Jobs becomes Overview (today's report) · Pending · Cancelled · Estimates. Technicians (new) holds Tech report · AI checks. Office (new) holds Dispatch · Complaints · Accounting weekly · Recruiting. That's 11 top tabs instead of 18. A family tab shows when any of its sub-tabs is granted, and a sub-tab shows only with its own grant. The sub-tab you were on is remembered per person.
