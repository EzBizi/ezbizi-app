Reports · Performance · choosing the dates

Jose, 2026-10-06, through Reports: "Custom date picker on former experimental / From date to date / Or specific date / Week is confussing what week is it ? Etc i should see the range / Maybe dropdown like google". What is there today: Day, Week and Month buttons, arrows, and a label that reads 2026-09-28 – 2026-10-04, so you have to work out for yourself which week that is.

Data: the server already takes any dates, on Reports' branch: a period around a day (weeks run Monday to Sunday), or from–to. One date is from = to, and it allows up to 92 days; past that it answers with a sentence. Every option shows the dates in words ("Mon, Sep 28 – Sun, Oct 4"), never 2026-09-28. The arrows step by whatever length is chosen: a week back, a month back, or ten days back for a ten-day range.

1One button that says the dates, opening a Google-style picker under it Recommended

The dates are always on the button in words: "This week · Mon, Oct 5 – today". Clicking it opens the picker under it, in the page, the way the calendar on a date field opens. On the left are the quick choices, each with the dates it means beside it, so "what week is it?" is answered before you click. On the right are two months: click one day for a single date, or two days for a range. Above them, From and To can be typed. The footer counts the days, and Apply runs the report. Why: this is the Google picker Jose described (quick choices, calendar, from–to), and the dates are visible both before and after choosing.

2Quick choices as chips, the dates spelled out under them, From–To for anything else

The quick choices are a row of chips, and under them a sentence always says what is showing: "Showing Mon, Sep 28 – Sun, Oct 4 (last week, Monday to Sunday)". Custom opens From and To fields in the row, the same date boxes used across the app, each with its own calendar, plus Just one day. Why: the simplest, with everything in sight. The cost is no range calendar: a range is two dates typed or picked one at a time.
