Customer Experience · Business Profile and Reviews

The Customer Experience session (2026-10-27): "3 layout options each, for Jose to pick, for the screens replacing Rodrigo's system": media, services and categories, review stats, phone check, other review sources, and a WhatsApp link. Options are numbered 1–18 across all six screens. Every name and number here is invented.

Screen 1 · Business Profile media

1 Media grid with type chips, Copy-to-locations in a SidePanel RECOMMENDED

  • One grid per listing: Logo and Cover pinned first, then photos and videos, with chips All · Photos · Videos · Logo · Cover.
  • Tick thumbnails, then Copy to locations opens a SidePanel listing every other listing as "Company · Branch" with a search and tick-all.
  • The panel shows what each target already has, so a copy never silently replaces a cover.
  • Upload is drag-and-drop on the grid, no dialog.

2 One table of all listings × media slots

Rows are listings, columns are Logo, Cover, photo count, video count, so gaps show at a glance and copying is "fill this column from row X". Great for auditing many branches, but you can't see the photos themselves without opening a row.

3 Today's per-listing page, plus a Copy button on each photo

The smallest change: keep one long page per listing, add ⋯ › Copy to locations on each item. Copying ten photos is ten trips, and there's no type filter.

Screen 2 · Services and categories

4 Categories as chips on top, services list grouped under them, Copy in a SidePanel RECOMMENDED

  • Primary category first (one), then additional categories as removable chips, with search to add.
  • Services are compact rows grouped by category: name, price type, description preview, edit in a SidePanel.
  • Copy to locations offers "Replace" or "Add missing only", and previews the per-listing diff before saving.

5 Matrix: services as rows, listings as columns, a tick per cell

Copying becomes ticking a column, and missing services stand out. It gets wide past six listings and descriptions/prices need a separate edit view.

6 Template set: edit one master list, push it to listings

Keep a company-level services template and apply it to branches. Clean for uniform branches, but per-branch differences (one branch doesn't sweep chimneys) become overrides that are easy to lose on the next push.

Screen 3 · Review stats, reply queue and AI drafting

7 Stats strip above a two-column queue: histogram and gauge left, reply queue right RECOMMENDED

  • KPI tiles: average, reviews this month, reply rate, AI replies sent; then histogram and average gauge per listing filter.
  • Reply queue on the right with chips Unreplied · Drafted · Sent, search, and each review showing its AI draft inline.
  • Draft all writes drafts for the filtered queue; nothing sends until you approve.
  • Send window shown and edited in place: approved replies go out Mon–Sat 8 am–6 pm, branch time.

8 Queue-first inbox, stats in a collapsible header

Reviews open like an inbox with the draft beside each one; stats fold into one line on top. Fastest for daily replying, but the histogram and gauge are hidden by default, so trends get missed.

9 Separate Stats and Replies tabs

A full dashboard tab and a full queue tab. Each has room to breathe, but the send window and batch drafting live away from the numbers they move.

10 One row per listing, three numbers side by side, a match chip RECOMMENDED

  • Chips All · Mismatch · Match, search, and "Company · Branch" on every row.
  • Fix opens a SidePanel showing which side changes, with the policy number preselected.

11 Exceptions only: a list of mismatches with a green "all others match" line

Shorter and calmer when things are right. But a listing that silently drops out of the check also drops out of view, and nobody can confirm a match by eye.

12 A phone chip on each listing card in the Info tab

No new tab: the check rides along where the number is edited. It's the least effort, but comparing all branches means opening each listing.

Screen 5 · Other review sources and the review funnel

13 Sources table plus a funnel card, both filtered by listing RECOMMENDED

  • Sources: Google, eKomi, Thumbtack as rows with connection chip, count, average and last sync.
  • Funnel: requests sent › opened › rated › posted publicly, per source, for the chosen period.
  • Reviews from every source flow into the Screen 3 queue with a source chip; only Google gets replies from here.

14 One card per source, funnel inside each card

Each source reads as its own story. With three sources it's fine; the combined funnel disappears, and you compare sources by scanning across cards.

15 Funnel only, sources as a filter chip row

The funnel is the page and sources are chips above it. Clean, but connection health (an expiring Thumbtack token) has nowhere obvious to live.

Screen 6 · WhatsApp chat link on each profile

16 A WhatsApp row in each listing's Info tab, with copy and a QR RECOMMENDED

  • Add to profile writes it as the chat link on Google; Copy and QR for flyers and vans.
  • A one-line list on top shows which listings have no link yet.
