Reports · Performance · room for what keeps being added

Jose, 2026-10-12, with a screenshot of Calls: "look at all of the available tabs and options and see how you can improve the UI, maybe add some more columns, cause the stuff we keep adding keeps overflowing, like with this screen, where stuff is a mess." Calls draws each call as a sentence with no column headings. The badges (direction, client, result) wrap to two lines in a 230 px box. Every row gets a second line for "Not transcribed yet" or "Claude: — · Grok: ETA". The middle of the screen is empty while the right edge is crowded.

Same in every option. Calls becomes a table with headings that stay put, like People's. Each fact gets its own column, so a new one adds a column instead of another pill in the same box. A row is one line. Its words, recording, numbers and both AIs' readings open on a tap, and nothing says "not transcribed" until somebody opens the row. The filters stay as they are (Result, Client, then Direction and Why under Missed), and the Claude / Grok switch moves into the bar, since it decides the Reason column. Every column is data the screen already has, and names and numbers here are invented.

1A table, and a row opens in place RECOMMENDED

Columns: Time · In/Out · Caller · Line called · Client · Result · Answered by · Reason ✦AI · Talk, then the play button. Line called is the line RingCentral rang (Bar's ask, which today shows only inside a missed call), so which ad or listing brought the call reads down a column. Reason is the selected AI's label, carrying the AI mark. Tapping a row opens it underneath: the transcript, the recording, both AIs' readings, the numbers, and Mark booked where someone may. Clicking a heading sorts by that column. A search box finds a caller, a number or a person.
