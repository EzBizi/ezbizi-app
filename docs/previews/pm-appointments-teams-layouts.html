209 · Project Management: several appointments per job, Repair teams tab, day colours

Jose with Shoval, 11-23, via the desk; Jobs builds the server. Three changes. (a) "Ready to schedule" can book one or several appointments, each with its own date and time, technicians or a repair team, and items, and the customer text lists them all. (b) A new Repair teams tab: pick a team to see its jobs, and its technicians' jobs, as a list and on the Field map, with a hover card for each job. (c) The status colour counts days in the current stage: 1 day green, 2 yellow, 3 or more red. All names are invented.

(a) Ready to schedule

A1 · One side panel of appointment cards, "+ Add appointment", text preview at the foot (recommended)

Each card has: date, time slot (8–11 / 11–2 / 2–5 or typed), an Assign switch (Technicians | Repair team) with its picker, and items picked from the job's parts and services, or added, with quantities. Cards can be duplicated or removed. The customer text at the foot updates live and is read-only, with "Send the text" ticked by default.

(b) Repair teams tab

B1 · Team picker, list on the left, Field map on the right, hover cards (recommended)

A seventh PM tab, "Repair teams". It has a team picker (SearchPickerField, with the team's colour) and a range of Today, This week or Custom. The list groups the jobs by day, each tagged "Team" or with the technician's name. The map uses Field's map: pins in the team colour, filled for team jobs and outlined for technicians' jobs. Hovering a pin or a row shows the card with number, type, customer, time, technicians, items and parts (with Received or On order). Clicking opens the job.
