Reports › Scoring › What the score weighs

A person's Scoring score is now one number out of 10: Performance's conversion rate plus the AI's seven checks on each call. Each part has a weight, and the weights add up to 100. These options set the weights. Every name here is invented.

Reports' server (051fa5e6): eight weights, each a whole number from 0 to 100, which must total exactly 100 or the save is refused. Defaults: Conversion 40, Greeted 5, Explained the price 5, and 10 each for the other five. Changing them needs the new Roles tick Change what the score weighs (reports.scoring_weights). Without it, a person's page still says what the score weighs.

1 A Weights panel beside the ranking RECOMMENDED

⚖ Weights sits in the Scoring header beside Score / Most improved, for those who may change it. It opens the app's side panel. A bar shows the split at a glance, and each part has a slider and a number, so you can drag or type. The total says 100 of 100 in green. Anything else is amber and says what is left ("95 of 100: add 5"), and Save waits until it's 100. Back to the defaults restores 40 / 5 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 5 / 10.

2 The split on the Scoring tab itself

A strip above the ranking shows the split for everybody: the bar and a legend. Those who may change it see Edit, which turns the strip into eight number boxes in two rows. It's always visible, but it takes room on every visit, and typing eight numbers is slower than dragging.

3 Two steps: conversion against the calls, then the calls among themselves

One slider splits the 100 between Conversion and How the calls went. Then the seven checks share the calls' part, and the rest rescale as you drag one, so it always adds up and nothing ever has to be refused. It's quick for the big decision, but the automatic rescaling can surprise you when you only meant to move one number.
