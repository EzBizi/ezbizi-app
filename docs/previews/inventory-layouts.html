Inventory · Stock, Items, Orders and Locations

Jose, 2026-10-01: “same for the whole inventory tab and sub tabs, this is pretty pathethic atm”

Answer with one desktop number and one phone number, for example “1, 4”. Each option draws Stock in full, and under it the other three tabs and one more state in the same style.

What is wrong today: Stock is one white card per item per place, two lines each. The same 6 mil liner is two cards (8 in the shop, 40 in the warehouse) and nothing adds them up. There are no columns, no money, no grouping by item or by place, and most of the screen is empty. The red Low on the shop’s 8 sits one row above 40 more in the warehouse: the reorder point belongs to the item, so that pill means “this shelf is under the item’s number” and reads as “buy more”. Items, Orders and Locations are the same two-line cards, and a location shows its name and kind but not what it holds or what it is worth.

Every option keeps General first in the strip (Jose’s placement, queue #8; General has its own brief and is not drawn here), judges an item by its total across every place (the server already answers that as low_items), opens records and flows in place or in a docked panel and never in a dialog, and keeps the actions that work today: Transfer, Count, Add item, Add location, Purchase order, Receive, Issue, Sale. Values and costs show only to roles with Inventory financials; without it those columns go and the screens still read complete. A dashed server tag marks what the server does not send today.

1One row per item, each place inside it Recommended

Four equal tiles are the filters: everything with its value, below reorder, out of stock, on order. One row per item with its total, its reorder point, what is already on order and its value; open a row to see each place, with Transfer and Count on that place’s own line. Transfer docks on the right and shows what both places hold now and after.

server One grouped read of stock: a row per item with its places, value and on-order, including items held nowhere. Today /inventory/stock is one row per place, carries no cost, and leaves out an item with no balance. Plus on-hand on /inventory/items, per-place totals on /inventory/locations, and a movements read for History. Below about 200 items the client can join the first three itself.

2A grid: items down, places across

Every quantity on one screen: one column per place, each item’s total, reorder point and value, and each place’s value along the bottom. Click a cell and the strip under the grid moves stock into that place, counts it or issues from it, without leaving the grid. Items edit in their cells, an order receives in its rows, and counting a place is a column you type into.

server Cost on each /inventory/stock row, or the client joins /inventory/items. The rest is client only up to about 200 items: the grid pivots stock, items and locations; edits use the existing PATCH; one transfer posts many lines; a count posts one adjustment per changed row. Van cells are not shaded: one reorder point per item cannot say what a van should carry, so a per-place minimum would be a new server field.
