Settings: Organization, Roles, Activity and API activity, redesigned

Jose, 2026-10-19: "u didnt finish revamping the whole settings tab, i see no changes on organization, activity - api activity / roles and permission". Option 1 in queue 110 only gave these four tabs the shared header. This proposal redesigns each of them. Every name here is invented.

1 Each tab becomes a list on the left and the selected item on the right RECOMMENDED

Organization. Today it's a long column of company cards with a menu on each. Instead:

  • Left: the tree of the organization, its companies and their branches, each with a count of people.
  • Right: whatever you picked:
    • its numbers: branches, people, phone numbers and connections;
    • its details: name, address and phone;
    • which settings it overrides;
    • its actions in one row: Edit, Add a branch and Archive.
  • Structure stays a button in the header.

Roles & permissions. Today it's a list of role cards, and editing one opens a narrow side panel of collapsible checkbox groups. Instead:

  • Left: the roles, with how many people hold each.
  • Right: the selected role as one grid:
    • a row per module, with columns for Open, Tabs, What they can do and Invite;
    • a half-filled box means some of a module's permissions are given; clicking a row opens that module's full list in place;
    • search and Compare with another role sit on top;
    • the people who hold the role are listed underneath.

Activity.

  • A table grouped by day, with columns When, Who, What, Where (company or branch) and Module.
  • Filters:
    • time;
    • who: a person, people, automations or EzBizi support;
    • module;
    • a search box for the name of a customer, lead or setting.
  • Opening a row shows what it was, then what it became in a panel on the right, plus a link to the thing itself.

2 Keep each tab's layout, but deeper

Here the four tabs keep their current layouts:

  • Organization stays a list of cards, with the numbers and an inline action row added to each card.
  • Roles keeps the side-panel editor, now full height and wider, with the grid from 1 inside it.
  • Activity and API activity get the filters and day groups from 1, but rows still open in a side panel instead of a pane beside the table.

This is smaller to build, but you keep scrolling past companies, and you can't see two roles side by side.

3 One page per tab, no left list

Everything is full width:

  • Organization becomes a table of companies and branches with their numbers.
  • Roles becomes one big grid, with roles as columns and modules as rows, so every role can be compared at once.
  • Activity and API activity are tables with the details opening under the row.

This is the best for comparing, but the grid gets wide past six roles, and it's hard to use on a phone.
