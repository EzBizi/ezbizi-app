Customer Experience › Complaints: every bad rating, and how far it got

Jose, 2026-10-20: "add a tab called Complaints which will show all the Bad Ratings that arrive on leads as well, ideally sort of like a table view like taskboard, that shows the progress of each bad rating, if it was solved or not, or in progress, etc." Every name here is invented.

One list covers three sources:

  • bad reviews (3★ or fewer);
  • bad ratings left on the website's sad-face form;
  • complaint calls, which the AI flags with a summary.

Each complaint is New, In progress, Solved or Can't be solved, and has an owner and notes. Someone who can manage complaints changes the status, the owner and the notes. Everyone else can only read.

1 A table, like Task Board, with Board one click away RECOMMENDED

  • Tabs across the top count each status: Open (New + In progress) · New · In progress · Solved · Can't be solved · All.
  • Filters: what kind, owner, branch and dates.
  • Each row shows when it came in, what kind it is, who it's from, what it says, the branch, the owner, the status and how many notes it has.
  • Opening a row shows a panel on the right with:
    • the whole review or call summary;
    • the status and owner, as pickers;
    • the notes, with a box to add one;
    • Open the review or Open the lead, which goes where it came from.
  • Table | Board switches to a column per status, where you drag a card to move it.
