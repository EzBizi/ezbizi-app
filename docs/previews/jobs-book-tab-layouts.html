Jose: "perhaps we ought to have a fully dedicated booking sub tab in jobs, and review every place that booking buttons are". One Book flow on its own Jobs tab: the customer (found or new), the address, services, a live availability grid that will not let a window go over its limit, the technician, the source and notes, then Create in one step. Every other Book button (Leads, Customers, Communication, Jobs list, Scheduling) opens this same flow with what it already knows filled in. The grid reads the area's real windows (scheduling/availability: capacity, taken, remaining); a full window is striped and cannot be picked, and the server refuses it too. Saving stays EzBizi first, then Workiz, as now.

1 One page: the form on the left, the grid on the right Recommended

Everything on one screen, no steps. The left column is filled top to bottom: customer (search; a match shows its card, or New customer), address (the customer's own addresses as chips, or a new one), services, source, notes. The right column is the area's next 7 days × windows, each cell showing spots left; it appears once the address gives a zip. Picking a cell lists the technicians covering that area with their load that day. The summary and Create job sit under the grid; Create lights only when customer, address and either a window or "Unscheduled" are set. Opened from another Book button, it is this page in a wide side panel.
