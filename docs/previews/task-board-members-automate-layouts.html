Task Board: your own boards, who is on them, and Automate

Jose's ask: anyone can create boards; a board's owner chooses who is on it, the way monday.com does; and each board gets easy automations, kept in step with the Automations tab but only about tasks. Every name here is invented.

How monday.com does it, carried over: every board has an owner, who adds people as Owner (may change the board and who is on it), Member (works the tasks) or Viewer (reads only). A board is Open, which everyone at its company or branch can find and join, or Private, which only its members see. Giving someone a task on a board you own asks whether to add them as a member. Role permissions still apply on top, and an Administrator sees every board.

Automate saves real rules: each recipe is an ordinary rule in the Automations engine, limited to this board, so it also shows in Automations › Your automations, where it can be edited in full. No second engine. The recipes: a task is created, its status changes or it is overdue, then assign, notify, send a Communication alert or move its status or group. Task Board builds the boards and members; Automations agrees how a rule is limited to one board; UI/UX builds these screens.
