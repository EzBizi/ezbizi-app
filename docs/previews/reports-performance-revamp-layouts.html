Reports · Performance, rearranged

Jose, 2026-10-06: "I believe we also need a ui revamp for the Reports Performance Tab, as we have added too many things and the UI is not very good for it anymore." What it holds today, in one scroll: the dates, seven figures, three panels that open between the figures and the table (Missed calls by reason; Existing-client calls with Claude's and Grok's labels; Not booked with Convert to booked and its history), the Workiz account controls, the people filter, the people table with each person's calls folded in, and a note on conversion.

Data: nothing new. Every option keeps every piece: the figures, the three call lists and their labels, the recordings and transcripts, Convert to booked, the people table and its sorting, the Workiz accounts, and the conversion note. What changes is where each one lives. In all of them: the Workiz account controls leave the page for a Sources side panel opened from the top, and a problem with them shows as one amber line at the top instead of a paragraph mid-page. The seven figures are always the same size.

1Three tabs: Overview, People, Calls Recommended

Overview answers "how did we do": the seven figures, then side by side the two lists that need somebody: Not booked (with Convert to booked) and Missed calls. Each shows its first few calls, with "See all" opening the Calls tab already narrowed to it. People is the table and its filter, alone. Calls is every call in one list, narrowed by chips (Missed, Existing, Not booked, New), with Claude's and Grok's reasons showing when they apply. Each call shows the first words of its transcript. A figure is a way in: tapping Missed calls opens Calls narrowed to missed. Why: each question gets a page of its own instead of panels opening into the middle of a table, and nothing that exists today is lost.
