AI Assistance · WhatsApp groups

From Meta / WhatsApp. Jose decided on 2026-10-06 that EzBizi replaces the Rodrigo bot's WhatsApp reports and alerts, sending them into new EzBizi WhatsApp groups. This screen is where those groups are set up: which report goes to which group, who is in it, and whether it is a real WhatsApp group yet.

Data: all on main (f505e9e5), for people who can manage AI Assistance. For each group: which report it receives (Financial reports, Local Services & GBP updates, Messages, Dispatch, and one Same-Day Report per branch), its WhatsApp name, its members (up to 8, WhatsApp's limit), whether each member has a phone on file, and once it is real, its invite link. Creating a real group needs Meta's Official Business Account, which can be requested from about October 15. Until then Make it a WhatsApp group is refused with a sentence, shown as written, and each member gets the report privately instead. A member with no phone gets nothing, and the screen says so.

1A card per group, and the reports with no group yet above them Recommended

A new WhatsApp groups tab beside AI, Library and Settings. Each group is a card: the report it gets, its name, how it is delivered now ("Sent privately to each member", or a real group with its invite link and Copy), and its members as chips. A member with no phone is a red chip, "no phone · won't receive". Add and remove members on the card; at 8 the Add chip turns off and says WhatsApp's limit. Above the cards, any report that has no group yet is listed with Make its group, so nothing Rodrigo used to send goes missing. Why: a handful of groups, each about who is in it. The card shows that and the one thing that can go wrong (a member who receives nothing) right where it is fixed.
