# Full UI review, web and phone — 2026-10-08

Jose, 2026-10-08: *"Make a full revision of the app both web and mobile modes to see if anything needs improving or
updating"*. UI/UX opened every module and every tab, on a desktop window (1440×900) and at phone width (390×844).
That was 22 modules plus Settings, Suggestions and Your account: 360 screens, each shot as it opens and again
scrolled down. It ran on a local copy filled with invented demo data
(review:seed-organization, leads/inventory/inspection demo seeders), so lists are short and Email, Phone, Google and
social accounts are not connected. Six reviewers read the screens and their findings were then checked. These were
**removed as artefacts of the test, not the app**: spinners caught by a slow local server (each re-shot loaded fine),
phone shots where an off-screen tab could not be reached, and tap-target sizes measured in a desktop browser (on a
real phone Flutter gives every chip a 48 px touch area).

The phone pass is the web app at phone width, which uses the same phone layouts as the apps. Things only the
installed apps do (push, location, camera, permissions) were not part of this pass.

## Across the app (UI/UX)

1. **Pickers look like search boxes.** Every searchable dropdown (SearchPickerField) shows a magnifier, so Language,
   Timezone, Units, "Any status", "All places", Company, Person and dozens more read as search fields. → UI/UX:
   a dropdown chevron, with search still inside.
2. **Nested tab rows look identical.** Marketing has three stacked rows of the same pills (Newsletter / Social /
   Web, then Posts / Accounts / QR codes, then Compose / Calendar), and so do Web Management (SEO › General …),
   Memberships and Scheduling › Settings. The Scheduling Settings sub-row even repeats the names of the row above
   (two "Availability", two "Schedule"). → UI/UX: a gallery for one second-level style.
3. **Organization-wide cards inside module Settings tabs.** "Business address" and "Mail server for sending"
   appear in Field › Settings, AI Assistance › Settings and Work Hours › Settings, and the counts there ("Search
   68 settings") are the whole organization's. → Settings owner.
4. **Empty states disagree.** Some sit centred, some top-left, some in cards. Many name a place as plain text with
   no link ("Settings › Numbers", "Settings › Integrations", "the Library tab", "Automations", "Roles &
   permissions"), and several have no next step at all. → UI/UX: one empty-state component with an optional
   action. Each module owner then supplies its button.
5. **Developer wording shown to users.** Examples: "Takes effect when scheduling ships", "when invoicing ships"
   (Settings); "Only things this system actually emits", "before the engine stops the cascade", "Rules are yours —
   they never need a new release" (Automations); "the widgets are dressed per site" (Web Management); "no
   credential is configured" (Reports); "a confirmation email, which is not wired up yet", "Permissions decide WHAT
   you may do" (Your account); "this is the whole answer rather than a step you are missing" (Work Hours); "Fetch
   new checks again straight away" (Leads). → each owner rewrites in plain words.
6. **Sibling tiles of different sizes.** Fleet › General (VEHICLES taller, "Nobody has the keys" alone on a row),
   Inventory › General (two tall, two short, then a half row), Monitoring › PC Specs, the Newsletter tiles on a
   phone (a hole mid-grid), and the Field tiles not filling the row. → owners, under the equal-tiles rule.
7. **Title Case where the app uses sentence case.** "Search by Zip Code", "Search by Area", "Calendar View"
   (Scheduling); the tabs "New Inspection" and "Generated Reports" (Inspection); "SUSPENDED" (Business Profile).
8. **Phone headers that never scroll away.** Settings, Organization, HR › Records and Automations › Build pin
   filters and descriptions that take a third of the screen. On Leads and Estimates an empty list scrolls the
   filters off and leaves a blank page.

## By module (owner)

blank in Money but "—" elsewhere. On a phone the Money chips run off the edge (Status is already a dropdown there).
Estimates: the empty state has no button, and on a phone its status chips are cut off.

**Customers** — Medium: customer numbers mix "C-1caa4562" with "C-00006", and phone numbers mix "+1 (954)
555-0123" with "305-555-0199". Franchise and email are cut off while the empty City column takes the space.

**Communication** — Medium: the Phone tab has a "Team" sub-tab and the Users tab is headed "Team". The Users text

account yet, so calls … reach nobody", and its KIND column is empty. Dial's empty state has no button to Settings.

**Human Resources** — Medium: an unlabeled "10" badge on every person in Records. Low: the empty

## What looked right

Overbooking (equal tiles, clear all-clear), Inspection's New Inspection and Builder, Newsletter Settings, Monitoring
Map and Camera Monitoring, HR Contracts and Invitations, the Services catalogue, the customers and jobs lists on a
phone, Roles & permissions, and every module's header and shell. No screen showed a raw key, "null", an HTTP code
or a red error box.
