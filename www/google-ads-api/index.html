Business model

EzBizi is sold as a monthly software subscription to service businesses — home services, trades, and multi-location franchises. They use it to manage customers, jobs, inspections, inventory, vehicles and staff in one system.

Revenue comes entirely from those subscriptions. EzBizi does not resell advertising, take a percentage of ad spend, manage campaigns as a service, or monetise data in any form. Google Ads API access exists for one purpose: to bring a customer's own advertising results into that customer's own CRM record.

What the tool does

The Leads module inside EzBizi. Its purpose is to close the gap between an advertising enquiry and the work that results from it.

A service business receives enquiries through Google Local Services Ads. Those enquiries live in Google's interface, while the resulting customer, job and technician assignment live in the CRM. Nobody can answer "what did this lead actually become?" without cross-checking two systems by hand.

The Leads module retrieves each customer's own Local Services lead and conversation data, plus their Google Ads reporting metrics, and shows them beside that business's existing records. An enquiry becomes a customer and a job in the same system, with the originating source preserved so the work can be attributed back to the advertising that produced it.

Who uses it

  • Owners and operations managers of service businesses running Local Services Ads.
  • Office and dispatch staff who answer enquiries and schedule the work.
  • Multi-location operators who need per-location attribution across several accounts.

Every user reaches the data through their own EzBizi login, governed by role-based permissions inside their own organization. It is not offered as a general-purpose Google Ads management tool and Google Ads data is never shown to anyone other than the account owner's own staff.

Authorization — each customer connects their own account

EzBizi is a multi-tenant platform. Each customer authorizes their own Google Ads account through a standard OAuth 2.0 authorization-code flow against EzBizi's own registered OAuth client. There is no shared credential, no manager-level access to accounts we were not granted, and no scraping of any kind.

1A signed-in EzBizi user opens the Leads tab and chooses Connect Google Ads.
2They are sent to Google's consent screen, branded EZBIZI LLC, requesting the adwords scope with offline access.
3They grant or decline. Declining changes nothing.
4EzBizi exchanges the code, encrypts the refresh token, and stores it scoped to that customer alone.
5The accounts the customer has granted are listed, and they choose which to link.
6Scheduled synchronisation retrieves that customer's leads and reporting on their behalf.
7They may disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored grant.

Offline access is requested because synchronisation runs on a schedule rather than only while somebody is watching the screen.

Read and write

Usage is predominantly read. There are two writes to Google. The first is replying to a text enquiry, which sends the message through the Local Services conversation for that lead, in the customer's own account. It happens when a person in the EzBizi interface writes the message and presses Send, or when the customer has switched on an automated first reply: a message the customer wrote themselves, sent when a new text enquiry arrives. EzBizi supplies no wording of its own, and calls and bookings are never replied to. Lead status is kept in EzBizi's own database and is never written back. A reply a person sends is recorded in that organization's audit log with the acting user, timestamp and target; an automated reply is marked as automated on the lead and recorded in that rule's run history.

The second is pausing or resuming one of the customer's own campaigns. It happens only when a person holding the Manage Google Ads permission presses Pause or Resume, and it changes the campaign's status and nothing else. No rule or schedule does it. Each change is recorded in that organization's audit log with the acting user, timestamp and campaign.

EzBizi does not create or remove campaigns, or change anything about a campaign other than pausing or resuming it; set or adjust bids or budgets; manage keywords, assets or creatives; or perform automated bulk mutation of any kind.

Why no narrower scope is requested

The Google Ads API publishes exactly one OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords. There is no read-only variant, so requesting it is the only way to read a customer's own lead and reporting data.

Request volume

  • Scheduled incremental synchronisation per connected account, using per-customer cursors so each run retrieves only what has changed since the last successful point.
  • Reporting queries are date-ranged and requested on demand or once daily — never polled.
  • Requests are rate-limited per customer, retried with exponential backoff, and stop on quota signals rather than retrying blindly.

Data handling

Per-customer isolation. Every stored record carries its owning organization. Reads are filtered and writes are stamped and verified at the data layer; cross-organization writes are refused outright.

Token storage. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with application-level encryption, masked on every serialisation, and are not retrievable through any API response or support view.

No client-side credentials. The OAuth client secret and all tokens remain server-side. Redirect URIs are derived from the application's own configured origin, never from request input.

No aggregation, no resale. Data retrieved for one customer is never combined with another's, sold, or used by EzBizi to train models. It is shared with one service provider: the text of Local Services messages and call transcripts goes to xAI (Grok), with data retention switched off on our account, to sort and summarise each enquiry and draft a reply that the business reviews before sending, and to assess how the business's own staff handled a call, for coaching. See the privacy policy.

Revocation. Disconnecting deletes the stored grant. Customers may also revoke access in their Google Account, which EzBizi detects and surfaces as a broken connection rather than failing silently.

Audit. Connection, disconnection and every write are recorded with the acting user and a timestamp.

Compliance

EZBIZI LLC accepts the Google Ads API Terms and Conditions and the Required Minimum Functionality expectations. The tool is used exclusively by the businesses that own the connected accounts, to view and act on their own advertising results.
