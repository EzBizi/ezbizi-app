EzBizi is field-service business software published by EZBIZI LLC. Chimney sweeps, garage-door installers, plumbers and similar trades run their customers, jobs, inspections, staff and vehicles in it. Social Media Management is the part of EzBizi that handles the posting those businesses already do.

What it does

A business connects the accounts it already owns and writes a post once, instead of signing into five apps and pasting the same thing five times. Sending is being built one network at a time — three of the nine send today, and the table below says exactly where each of the others has got to.

How a business uses it

  1. Connect. Someone with permission opens Marketing → Social Media Management → Accounts, chooses a network, and signs in to that network themselves. The network's own consent screen is what grants access. EzBizi never asks for a password to a social account.
  2. Write. They compose one post — text, and images or video — in EzBizi.
  3. Choose where it goes. They tick the connected accounts it should go to. A network that cannot take the post says so instead of failing quietly.
  4. Publish or schedule. It goes now, or at a time they set in their own timezone, to the networks that can take it yet. The calendar shows what is queued and what has gone.

Which networks

Eight can be connected: Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, Threads, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube, X and Bluesky. TikTok is the ninth, and a business can connect it once TikTok has reviewed EzBizi's app. A business connects only the ones it uses; the rest sit in the catalogue until it does.

Connecting and publishing are different things, and we would rather show you the gap than let you find it. Three networks send today. For the other six, the table says exactly where each has got to — when sending is proven for one, it works without reconnecting.

Whose accounts these are

The connected account always belongs to the business that connected it, not to EzBizi. Where EzBizi posts, it posts as that business's own account, on their instruction, with access they granted and can withdraw.

Access is bound to the part of the business that connected it. A company connecting its Facebook Page shares that Page with the locations under it, and with the group above it — never with a sibling company. Two businesses using EzBizi can never see or post to each other's accounts.

Disconnecting

Any account can be disconnected in EzBizi at any time, from the same screen it was connected on. Disconnecting deletes the stored access immediately. Because the grant lives with the network as well, we also tell people to remove EzBizi in that network's own settings — that is what revokes it at the source.

If someone removes EzBizi from the network's side first, the connection is marked as revoked here too, so nobody is left looking at an account that says "connected" and quietly is not.

What is stored

The access token, the account's id, its display name and handle, and the posts written in EzBizi along with where each one was sent and what the network answered. Tokens are encrypted and are never shown on screen or returned by our API. EzBizi does not keep a copy of a network's feed.

More detail is in the privacy policy, and deletion is covered on the delete account page.
