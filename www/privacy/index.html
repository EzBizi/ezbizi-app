Where EzBizi runs

This notice covers EzBizi wherever you use it: in a browser at app.ezbizi.com, in the Windows desktop app, and in the Android and iPhone apps. They are one product against one account, and they are treated identically here.

The apps ask for no device permissions. They request network access and nothing else — no camera, no photo library, no location, no microphone, no contacts, no calendar. Nothing on your phone or computer is read outside the app, and there is no background collection when you are not using it. If that ever changes, the operating system will ask you first, and this page will say so before it does.

What we collect

Three kinds of information, and it is worth keeping them apart:

  • Your account. Your name, work email, role and permissions, sign-in credentials, active sessions, any authenticator enrolment, and a profile photo if you upload one.
  • What your organization enters. Customers, jobs, estimates and invoices, inspection reports, inventory, vehicles, tasks, and files and photos attached to any of them. Some of it is personal data about other people — your customers, your staff — and your organization decides what goes in.
  • Records the service produces itself. Technical and security logs, audit entries showing who changed what, and error diagnostics. We keep these because an account we cannot investigate is an account we cannot protect.

Staff records go further where an organization uses EzBizi for its own people: home address, pay basis, worked hours from clock-in, onboarding documents and signed agreements. Clock-in records when somebody worked. It does not record where they were — EzBizi collects no location data of any kind.

Calls and messages

An organization can connect a business phone number to EzBizi. Where it does, we process the number called, the direction and time, how long it lasted, and voicemail and any call recording the organization chooses to keep, so that the call can be attached to the customer it was about. Telephony is carried by Twilio, which handles the call itself.

Recording a call is the organization’s decision and the organization’s legal responsibility — some places require every party’s consent. EzBizi provides the capability; it does not provide the consent.

Connected social accounts

If you connect a social network (for example TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, Pinterest, Threads, or Bluesky), we receive the tokens and profile identifiers that network issues so EzBizi can compose, schedule, and publish posts you authorize. We use that access only to provide the Social Media Poster and related account connection. We do not sell that access. You can disconnect a network in EzBizi; you should also revoke the app in that network’s own settings.

Who sees it

Data belongs to the organization that entered it. People in that organization see what their role and assignments allow, and access is enforced on the server rather than hidden in the interface. Two organizations using EzBizi can never see each other’s records.

We do not sell personal information, we do not use it to train anything, and we do not use it to advertise to anyone.

Who we rely on

We keep this list short on purpose, and these are all of them:

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, storage and encrypted backups, in the United States.
  • Amazon SES — email we send on the service’s behalf.
  • Twilio — calls and messages, where an organization has connected a number.
  • The social networks you connect, and only the ones you connect, as described above.

EzBizi is hosted in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information is processed there.

Retention and deletion

We keep records while the organization has an account, and for a limited period afterward as security, backups and legal obligations require. You can ask us to export or erase personal data at the address above.

How to delete your account and your data is a page of its own — what gets erased, what has to be kept and why, and how long a deleted record can survive in a backup. Start there; it answers more than this paragraph can.

Children

EzBizi is business software sold to organizations. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Keeping it safe

Traffic is encrypted in transit, and backups are encrypted at rest and held off-site, with restores verified rather than assumed. Access tokens for connected accounts are encrypted and are never displayed or returned by our API. No system is perfect, and we would rather say that than imply otherwise.
