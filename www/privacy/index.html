EzBizi is a field-service CRM operated by EZBIZI LLC, 800 SE 4th Ave Ste 107, Hallandale Beach, FL 33009, United States. Contact: contact@ezbizi.com.

What we collect

When you or your organization uses EzBizi we process account details (name, work email, role), customer and job records you enter, and technical logs needed to run the service.

Connected social accounts

If you connect a social network (for example TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, Pinterest, Threads, or Bluesky), we receive the tokens and profile identifiers that network issues so EzBizi can compose, schedule, and publish posts you authorize. We use that access only to provide the Social Media Poster and related account connection. We do not sell that access. You can disconnect a network in EzBizi; you should also revoke the app in that network’s own settings.

Who sees it

Data belongs to the organization that entered it. People in that organization see what their role and assignments allow. We use subprocessors to host the application (including cloud infrastructure and email delivery). We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We keep records while the organization has an account and for a limited period afterward as needed for security, backups, and legal obligations. You can ask us to export or erase personal data at the address above.

Changes

We will update this page when the product or our practices change. This notice is provided so our integrations can show a public policy; it is not legal advice.
