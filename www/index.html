The platform

Every module, one record

Customers, field work, inspections, inventory and reporting ship as one product — not five tools stitched together and hoping to agree.

Customers

One record per customer, with every job, message and document attached to it.

Coverage & capacity

Set the areas each location covers and how much work a day can take, by slot and by weekday.

Field operations

Know where the work is happening and how it's progressing, without calling around.

Forms & reports

Capture what happened on site and turn it into something the customer can read.

Insight

Dashboards that answer the questions you'd otherwise export a spreadsheet to answer.

Growth

Track where new work comes from — down to the advert that produced the enquiry. Connect a Google Ads account and its Local Services enquiries arrive as leads, each carrying the calls and messages that came with it. Connect Thumbtack and its enquiries arrive the same way, carrying the messages that came with them, and you reply from the same screen. An AI can read the conversation and draft a reply, which a person reads and sends.

Advertising & business profiles

Connect the Google Ads and Business Profile accounts each location already owns. Ads brings its Local Services enquiries in as leads; reading and answering Business Profile reviews is waiting on Google to grant us API access. How it works

Communication

Your business numbers in one place — dial, texts and voicemail, with each role given the company’s lines rather than one number at a time.

Social media

Connect the accounts each location already owns and write a post once. Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads and Pinterest send today; posting to the others is still being proven. How it works

Monitoring

See the computers your organization runs — what is online, how each one is doing, and where it sits on a floor plan you draw. Screen capture, live view and remote control are there for the roles you grant them to. What is recorded

Newsletter

Write a mailing in blocks or straight HTML, see the month it goes out on, and send it through your own Mailchimp account — your list, your sending reputation.

Web management

Your WordPress site takes its phone, address, hours and licence numbers from here, so one change reaches every page — and each site keeps its own answers. Add the chat bubble and contact form in your own brand colour, and the enquiries they take arrive in Leads.

Inventory

Stock counted per item and per location, with what was bought, moved and used on a job.

Fleet

Every vehicle on the register: who has the keys, when insurance lapses, what service is due.

Inspections

Build the form once, then every visit produces the same report — findings, photos and all.

People

Hours, onboarding and records for the team, handled in the same system as the work.

Training

Write the guide, set the test, give it to the people who need it, and see who still owes what.

Automations

When something happens — a lead arrives, a call is missed, a job changes status, stock runs low — EzBizi runs the steps you set: send a text, open a follow-up job, assign it, email a saved report or tell the administrators. Add conditions to narrow a rule, or run it on a schedule.

AI & the API

Make an API key and an AI like Claude, or your own script, works in EzBizi as you — through an MCP server that reaches the same API the app uses, with your permissions and nothing more. A key can read only, read and edit, or also delete. Every change made with a key is logged with each field as it was before, and any key can be revoked.

Reports

Build a report from your own records — start from any of eleven parts of the business, from jobs and customers to fleet and inventory, pull in the columns from what they connect to, filter it and save it by name. Running a saved report and building one are separate permissions, so you can let somebody run what you built without letting them change it.

Multi-location

One account. Every location.

Most software assumes you run one site. EzBizi assumes you don't.

  • Separate where it matters — each location keeps its own teams, work and numbers.
  • Access that fits the org — give someone a few locations, not the whole company.
  • Shared where it helps — a customer served by two locations is still one customer.
  • A view of the whole — compare locations, or look at the group as one business.

FAQ

Questions we get first

What is EzBizi?

EzBizi is a field-service CRM for organizations that run work across many locations. Customers, jobs, inspections, inventory, vehicles and reporting live in one system, on the same record.

Who is EzBizi for?

Service businesses with more than one company, franchise or location — field teams, office staff and leadership working from the same records rather than a stack of separate tools.

Does EzBizi support multiple locations?

Yes. Each location keeps its own teams, work and numbers. Access can cover a few locations or the whole group. A customer served by two locations is still one customer.

Does EzBizi connect to Google?

Yes, and each location connects its own accounts rather than ours. A Google Ads account can be connected to bring Local Services enquiries in beside the customers and jobs they become. A Google Business Profile can be connected too, though reading and answering its reviews is waiting on Google to grant Business Profile API access. You authorise it yourself, you see only your own data, and you can disconnect at any time. How we use the Google Ads API.

What can I run EzBizi on?

Any modern browser at app.ezbizi.com, and a Windows desktop app that installs without an administrator and keeps itself up to date. The Android app is on Google Play and the iPhone app is on the App Store. See the downloads.

Can an AI work in EzBizi?

Yes. Each person can make an API key in their account and connect an AI like Claude, or a script, through EzBizi's MCP server. It works as that person, with exactly their permissions, and a key can be limited to reading. Administrators decide which roles may make keys, and every change made with a key is logged.

How do we get started?

Talk to us at contact@ezbizi.com, or open Work with us if you are already on the team. The product runs at app.ezbizi.com.
