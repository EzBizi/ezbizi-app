Multi-location

One account. Every location.

Most software assumes you run one site. EzBizi assumes you don't.

  • Separate where it matters — each location keeps its own teams, work and numbers.
  • Access that fits the org — give someone a few locations, not the whole company.
  • Shared where it helps — a customer served by two locations is still one customer.
  • A view of the whole — compare locations, or look at the group as one business.
Wherever the work is

  • Nothing re-typed — what happens in the field is already in the office.
FAQ

Questions we get first

What is EzBizi?

EzBizi is a field-service CRM for organizations that run work across many locations. Customers, jobs, inspections, scheduling, billing and reporting live in one system, on the same record.

Who is EzBizi for?

Service businesses with more than one company, franchise or location — field teams, office staff and leadership working from the same records rather than a stack of separate tools.

Does EzBizi support multiple locations?

Yes. Each location keeps its own teams, work and numbers. Access can cover a few locations or the whole group. A customer served by two locations is still one customer.

How do we get started?

Talk to us at contact@ezbizi.com, or open Work with us if you are already on the team. The product runs at app.ezbizi.com.
