Job Responsibilities

The Sales Executive's responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

• Follow up on estimates provided to customers to convert them into booked jobs
• Conduct over-the-phone sales and close pending opportunities
• Manage and handle all sales-related calls, inquiries, and follow-ups
• Assist clients and the field team with financing options and related paperwork
• Maintain accurate records of sales activity, follow-ups, and outcomes in the CRM
• Meet or exceed the sales targets and conversion goals set by the Company
• Coordinate with the field and office teams to ensure a smooth handoff from sale to service
• Perform additional sales and administrative tasks as reasonably assigned
The Employee is expected to maintain professional conduct and represent the {{organization.name}} brand positively in every customer interaction. Strong communication, organization, attention to detail, and a customer-first attitude are required.
