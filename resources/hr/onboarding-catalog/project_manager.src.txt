Office Project Manager – Role & Responsibilities

The Project Manager plays a critical leadership role in operational control, inventory management, sales support, repair scheduling, customer satisfaction, and performance tracking. This position ensures organization, accountability, profitability, and consistent operational standards across all repair operations.

1. Inventory Management

The Project Manager is fully responsible for ensuring that the inventory CRM system is accurate, updated, and properly maintained at all times.

Responsibilities include:

• Updating the inventory CRM for every:
– Part issued to technicians
– Part sold directly to customers
• Recording all inventory purchases immediately upon purchase
• Assigning the correct job number to every item used or sold to ensure accurate tracking
• Ensuring all data entries are completed in real time
• Maintaining system accuracy so the CRM can generate reliable:
– Weekly reports
– Monthly reports
– Quarterly reports
– Annual reports
Inventory accuracy is mandatory and directly impacts financial tracking and profitability reporting.

2. Parts Ordering & Purchasing Control

The Project Manager is responsible for monitoring inventory levels and maintaining proper stock quantities.

Responsibilities include:

• Reviewing inventory quantities regularly
• Identifying items that require reordering
• Placing purchase orders as needed
• Obtaining owner approval for any order exceeding $500
• Managing custom or special part orders requested by technicians
For custom or special orders:

• Technicians must notify the Project Manager immediately after a client deposit is collected
• Orders must be processed promptly to avoid project delays

3. Repair Team Scheduling

The Project Manager is responsible for managing and optimizing the repair team schedule.

Responsibilities include:

• Scheduling all deposit-approved repair jobs
• Ensuring repair teams remain consistently busy
• Managing workload distribution efficiently
• Minimizing downtime between projects
• Coordinating with office staff for scheduling adjustments when needed

4. Upsell Support

The Project Manager plays an active role in increasing revenue through upsell support.

Responsibilities include:

• Reviewing technician estimates before they leave job sites
• Assisting in developing upsell strategies
• Providing real-time support through internal communication channels
• Ensuring no job is finalized without evaluating upsell opportunities
This structure is intended to increase average ticket value and improve closing ratios.

5. Project Photo Documentation

The Project Manager is responsible for ensuring consistent and professional project documentation.

Responsibilities include:

• Collecting high-quality before and after photos for every completed project
• Ensuring photos are clear, professional, and marketing-ready
• Uploading all project photos to the designated Monday.com board
• Tracking repair team performance using visual documentation
• Maintaining a structured photo database for marketing and quality control purposes
Professional photo documentation is mandatory.

6. Review Performance Tracking & Reputation Management

The Project Manager must actively monitor and manage review performance across all locations.

Responsibilities include:

• Updating the weekly review tracking Excel sheet
• Monitoring review growth per location
• Reporting to the owner if technicians are not generating sufficient reviews
• Maintaining a minimum growth target of 20 new reviews per location per week

Negative Reviews Management

For any negative review:

• The Project Manager must cooperate with the Office Supervisor to investigate the issue
• Review job photos, documentation, and technician reports
• Communicate internally to determine responsibility and corrective action
• Provide the office with a clear resolution plan (redo work, partial refund, full refund if necessary)
• Ensure follow-up is completed promptly
• Take proactive steps to resolve the customer's bad experience and work toward review revision or removal when appropriate
Reputation management is a priority and must be handled daily without delay.

7. Complaint Management & Resolution

The Project Manager will work alongside the office team using a dedicated Monday.com complaint board.

Responsibilities include:

• Investigating complaints immediately
• Reviewing job documentation and photos
• Communicating with technicians and repair teams
• Providing clear resolution instructions to the office team
• Ensuring all complaint cases are addressed daily
No complaint should remain unresolved without active follow-up.

8. Repair Team Performance Reporting

The Project Manager must generate a detailed weekly repair performance report including:

• Total sales per job
• Inventory cost per job
• Employee labor costs
• Overall profitability per project
• Combined sales and production percentage analysis
The purpose of this report is to:

• Track repair team performance
• Ensure the company remains within budget
• Maintain a maximum combined target of 25% for sales and production costs
• Identify inefficiencies and recommend improvements

Role Summary

The Office Project Manager is the operational backbone of the repair department. This position directly impacts:

• Revenue growth
• Cost control
• Inventory accuracy
• Scheduling efficiency
• Customer satisfaction
• Online reputation
• Team accountability
• Profit margins
Strong organization, daily oversight, proactive communication, and leadership are required for success in this role.
