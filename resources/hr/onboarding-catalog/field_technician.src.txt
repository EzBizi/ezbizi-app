• Present estimates clearly and confidently
• Follow the required upsell process on every job

• Use CRM and internal systems properly
• Maintain clean and professional job sites
• Represent the company in a professional manner at all times

Successful completion of the training program is a condition of continued employment. The Employer reserves the right to extend the training period if the Employee has not demonstrated sufficient proficiency.

Training Compensation: The training period shall be fully compensated at the Employee's agreed-upon rate of pay.

5. COMPANY FINES & DEDUCTIONS

The Employee may be subject to the following fines, each charged at up to $100 per occurrence at the Company's discretion, for violations that affect company operations, customer satisfaction, or legal protection:

• Client cancellation due to lack of communication
• No-call / no-show for a scheduled job
• Failure to attend a scheduled Zoom meeting
• Customer leaves a negative review due to poor service or unprofessional behavior, and the company was unable to resolve or recover the situation
• Missing inspection report
• Closing a job without completing required checklist items (e.g., missing client signature, missing photos), especially if it results in a dispute, chargeback, or stopped payment
Before any fine is charged or any commission is withheld for the conduct described above, the Company will first issue the Employee three (3) documented warnings. Only after a third documented warning may the Company charge a fine or withhold commission for the same category of violation.

7. VEHICLE, EQUIPMENT RESPONSIBILITY & ATTENDANCE

(a) Clock-In / Clock-Out Requirement

The Employee is required to clock in and clock out at the company office daily. Failure to properly clock in or clock out may result in disciplinary action. Accurate time records are essential for payroll processing and compliance with applicable labor laws.

(b) Company Vehicle

The Employee acknowledges receipt of a company vehicle and agrees to:

• Conduct a full inspection upon receipt and sign off on its condition
• Be fully responsible for the proper treatment and cleaning of the vehicle
• Wash the vehicle at least twice per month to ensure it remains clean and presentable while in service
Any costs incurred by the Employee for washing, cleaning, or maintaining the vehicle will be reimbursed by the Company.

Company vehicles are GPS-tracked at all times.

Gas & Fuel Policy

All fuel expenses incurred in connection with the Employee's job duties and use of the company vehicle shall be paid in full by the Company. The Employee will be provided with a company fuel card (or reimbursed in full upon submission of fuel receipts) for all work-related fuel costs. The fuel card or reimbursement is strictly for business use; any personal use of the fuel card is prohibited and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

(c) Tools & Equipment

The Employee will receive a full set of tools (to be itemized in the attached checklists) and must sign a confirmation of receipt. The Employee is responsible for:

• Replacing any missing tool at their own expense
• Keeping all tools clean and well-maintained, including vacuum equipment and brushes
• Performing maintenance such as brush replacements, when approved by the manager
• Ensuring no technician arrives at a client's home with dirty or unprofessional equipment

8. UNIFORMS

The Employee will receive the following uniform items:

• 3 polo shirts
• 1 jacket
• 1 hat

9. JOB SCHEDULING, COMMUNICATION & BEHAVIOR

The Employee must:

• Notify the client with an ETA before the appointment window
• Inform the office if there is no answer from the client before the appointment time
Any missed appointment caused by failure to follow these steps may result in a fine as outlined in Section 5.

10. QUALITY ASSURANCE & REDO POLICY

The Employee is expected to perform every job at a top-quality standard reflecting the Company's reputation.

• The Employee is responsible only for workmanship failures directly caused by the Employee's own performance.
• The Employee is not responsible for failures resulting from materials, manufacturer defects, weather conditions, or other causes outside the Employee's control.
• Before any financial responsibility is imposed, the Company will provide up to three (3) forgiveness incidents for qualifying workmanship-related callbacks.
• Where the Employee is responsible, the Employee must return and redo the work at no additional charge. If the client refuses to have the Employee return, the Company may assign another technician to complete the redo.

11. PAYMENT HANDLING & CASH POLICY

The Company does not accept cash unless in emergency situations where the client has no alternative payment method. In such cases, the Employee must:

• Deposit the cash into the company's bank account, or
• Wire/Zelle the funds to the company immediately

Sales Tax Collection

The Employee is responsible for charging sales tax where required.

Credit Card Processing

All payments must be collected using credit card tap/swipe. Manual entries are prohibited unless necessary. Any fees incurred due to manual entry will be deducted from the Employee's earnings.

12. DOCUMENTATION & CLOSING PROCEDURES

The Employee must:

• Upload the inspection report, before and after photos, and all job documentation into the CRM system (Workiz)
• Send the closing link to the company group chat immediately after job completion
If any of the following are missing — signatures, photos, report, or estimate — the Employee may be subject to a fine as outlined in Section 5.

Client Signature Requirement

The Employee must collect client signatures at all three stages:

• Inspection Report
• Estimate Approval
• Invoice Completion
This is critical to protect the company against disputes, chargebacks, and legal claims.

13. COMPUTER AND ACTIVITY MONITORING

The Employee acknowledges and agrees that the Company reserves the right to monitor, access, and record any activity conducted on company-owned computers, devices, vehicles, and systems at any time. This includes, but is not limited to, internet usage, software applications, email communications, GPS tracking on company vehicles, and any other digital activity.

The Employee understands that there is no expectation of privacy when using company equipment or networks, and such monitoring may occur without prior notice. All data generated on company equipment remains the property of the Employer.

Field Monitoring & Recording

The Employee acknowledges and agrees that the Company is permitted to monitor and record the Employee while on the field during any communication with clients, whether by phone or in person. Such monitoring and recording is conducted solely for purposes of quality assurance, service improvement, training, and compliance verification. The Employee consents to such monitoring as a condition of employment.

14. USE OF IMAGE AND LIKENESS

The Employee authorizes the Employer to use the Employee's name, photograph, and likeness for legitimate business purposes, including but not limited to: the company website, internal systems, employee directories, marketing materials, social media, and related business platforms. This authorization remains in effect during and after the term of employment unless revoked in writing by the Employee.

15. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Employee acknowledges that during the course of employment, the Employee may have access to confidential and proprietary information belonging to the Employer, including but not limited to: customer lists, pricing structures, business strategies, marketing plans, operational procedures, financial data, software systems, and trade secrets (collectively, "Confidential Information").

The Employee agrees to:

• Not disclose, publish, or otherwise share any Confidential Information with any third party during or after employment without the prior written consent of the Employer.
• Not use any Confidential Information for personal benefit or for the benefit of any third party.
• Return all Confidential Information, documents, materials, keys, equipment, and company property upon termination of employment.
• The obligations set forth in this Section shall survive the termination of this Agreement and remain in effect indefinitely.

16. PROHIBITION OF SIDE JOBS, THEFT & MISUSE OF COMPANY RESOURCES

(a) Prohibition of Side Jobs and Job Diversion

The Employee is strictly prohibited from performing any chimney, fireplace, HVAC, or related home service work outside of their duties for {{organization.name}}, whether for personal gain or for the benefit of any third party. This includes, but is not limited to:

• Performing unauthorized or "off-the-books" work for clients, leads, or contacts obtained through or during the course of employment with {{organization.name}}
• Diverting, redirecting, or referring any customer, lead, or job opportunity away from {{organization.name}} to the Employee personally or to any third party
• Using company time, equipment, vehicles, tools, uniforms, or branding to perform work that is not authorized by the Company

(b) Prohibition of Theft and Financial Misconduct

The Employee shall not engage in any form of theft, embezzlement, or financial misconduct, including but not limited to:

• Collecting payments from customers and failing to remit them to the Company
• Accepting cash, tips, or other compensation from customers without proper documentation and disclosure to the Company
• Falsifying invoices, estimates, time records, inspection reports, or any other company documentation
• Misappropriating company funds, tools, equipment, materials, or any other company property

(c) Misuse of Company Vehicles

Company vehicles are provided exclusively for authorized business use. The Employee shall not use the company vehicle for any unauthorized purpose, including but not limited to performing side jobs, personal commercial activities, or any work not sanctioned by {{organization.name}}.

(d) Remedies and Right to Legal Action

The Employee acknowledges that any violation of this Section constitutes a material breach of this Agreement and may result in:

• Immediate termination of employment without notice
• The Company's right to pursue civil litigation against the Employee for the recovery of all damages, lost revenue, and profits resulting from the Employee's unauthorized activities
• The Company's right to seek injunctive relief to prevent ongoing or further violations
• The Company's right to report criminal conduct (including theft, embezzlement, or fraud) to the appropriate law enforcement authorities for criminal prosecution
• Recovery of all reasonable attorneys' fees, court costs, and expenses incurred by the Company in enforcing this Section
The Employee agrees that any revenue, payments, or compensation received from unauthorized side jobs or diverted work performed during the term of employment rightfully belongs to {{organization.name}}, and the Company shall be entitled to recover such amounts in full.

17. NON-COMPETE & NON-SOLICITATION

(a) Non-Compete

This provision applies only if the Company has provided training to the Employee. Where it applies, then during the term of employment and for a period of ninety (90) days following the termination of employment (regardless of the reason for termination), the Employee agrees not to, directly or indirectly, engage in, become employed by, consult for, or have any ownership interest in any business that provides chimney, fireplace, HVAC, or similar home services within a fifty (50) mile radius of any {{organization.name}} operating location where the Employee was assigned.

(b) Non-Solicitation

For a period of two (2) years following termination, the Employee agrees not to:

• Solicit, contact, or attempt to divert any customer or client of the Employer with whom the Employee had contact during employment
• Solicit, recruit, or encourage any employee of the Employer to leave their employment
• Solicit any past {{organization.name}} vendors or staff

(c) Remedies

The Employee acknowledges that a breach of this Section would cause irreparable harm to the Employer. In the event of a breach or threatened breach, the Employer shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and pursue civil litigation, including monetary damages and reasonable attorneys' fees. The Employee acknowledges that this clause is necessary to protect {{organization.name}}'s investment in training, client base, and proprietary systems, and agrees that it shall be interpreted to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

18. TERMINATION

(a) Notice of Resignation

The Employee must provide at least thirty (30) days' written notice prior to leaving the Company.

(b) At-Will Termination

The Employer may terminate the Employee at any time, with or without cause. The Employer reserves the right to provide pay in lieu of notice at its sole discretion.

(c) Immediate Termination

The Employer may terminate the Employee immediately and without notice for any of the following reasons:

• Dishonesty, fraud, theft, or embezzlement
• Gross misconduct or insubordination
• Violation of company policies or safety procedures
• Unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information
• Conviction of or plea to a felony or crime of moral turpitude
• Failure to perform assigned duties after written warning
• Being under the influence of drugs or alcohol during working hours
• Damage to company property, equipment, or reputation
• Performing unauthorized side jobs or diverting company business (as outlined in Section 16)

(d) Final Pay

Upon termination, the Employee shall receive all earned wages and any accrued but unused benefits to the extent required by applicable state law.
